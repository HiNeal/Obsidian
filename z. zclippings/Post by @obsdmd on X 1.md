Obsidian 1.13 is now available to everyone on desktop and mobile!

\- Added search and keyboard navigation to settings

\- Images are easier to resize and zoom

\- iOS share sheet lets you save content from other apps without opening Obsidian

\- Many bug fixes and performance

---

Drag to resize images. Click an image or its zoom button to expand it. You can navigate between all images in the current file, and drag to pan.

In Live Preview you can select images with the keyboard, resize with + and -, and copy, cut, or delete them directly.

---

Settings have been completely revamped with keyboard navigation, search, and new APIs for plugin developers.

On desktop the Settings panel now opens in a separate window (you can disable this).
