The flow diagram illustrates how plastic bottles are recycled.

==Overall==, there are 9 distinct steps in plastic-bottle recycling. Roughly speaking, plastic bottles are collected, sorted, broken down, and finally made into new products.

The process begins with the collection of used plastic bottles from homes, businesses, and other sites. They are then transported by truck to the recycling center for the further sorting process. At the center, plastic bottles are manually sorted and separated from irrelevant items, with recyclable bottles set apart for the following procedure. Those bottles are subsequently compressed into blocks and then crushed into small pieces, which are later washed in a container to remove all residues.

Once cleaned, those fragments are then sent into a machine to produce plastic pellets, and those pellets are heated afterward so that they become the raw materials for new products. Finally, end products are molded in various forms: plastic bottles, pencils, shirts, bags, and others. Those products then enter the market to fulfill their missions in people's daily lives, potentially to be recycled someday in the future.
