# 高糖食品征税 

## 题目

Many manufactured food and drink products contain high levels of sugar, which causes many health problems. Sugary products should be made more expensive to encourage people to consume less sugar. Do you agree or disagree?

## 范文

It is true that processed foods and beverages with excessive amounts of sugar contribute to various illnesses. However, I completely disagree with the proposal to raise the price of sugary products; in my opinion, it can generate multiple undesirable consequences, and we can adopt several alternatives to address the health-related issues.

The government's interference in sugary products’ prices, in my opinion, could bring about many unintended and negative consequences. Firstly, families with a limited budget may not be able to afford a variety of food and drinks that contain sugar. For example, we can imagine a poor mother's sorrow when she struggles between the desire to buy her little daughter a birthday cake and the frustration from the prohibitive price. Secondly, once the sugar ratio in a product becomes an influential factor in its price, some manufacturers might deliberately put gratuitous sugar content into the food so that extra profits can be guaranteed. This may be counterproductive to handling the health issue mentioned in the task, giving rise to harmful diseases, especially to people with a generous budget but limited awareness of the saccharin in their desired food.

There are many other options that can better tackle the health-related problem mentioned in the task. Firstly, compared to an abrupt price increase in sugary products, health talks in supermarkets that shed light on the potential risk of consuming sugary products might function better. Secondly, the salesmen/women in the stores can also provide health-related suggestions when customers show potential interest in purchasing food with excessive amounts of sugar. Last but not least, labels on sugary food and drinks should be informative enough to alert the customers to the inherent dangers, increased vulnerability to diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and heart attack, to name a few. Since many people flinch at cigarettes with alarming slogans on the covers, we can also expect them to shun sugary products with similar warning signs.

In conclusion, I argue that a price rise for sugary foods is liable to invite various problems, and we can resort to plenty of alternatives to make sure that people steer clear of the risk of excessive sugar consumption.

# 收入平等影响

## 题目

Write about the following topic:

The best way to make society happier is to ensure that the richest and poorest people have the smallest differences in earnings.

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

## 范文

It is often claimed that the most effective approach to maximizing collective well-being is to ==minimize the income disparity== between the wealthiest and the most impoverished members of society. While narrowing extreme wealth inequality undeniably alleviates poverty and social tension, I completely disagree that it represents the paramount path to a happier society; ==an obsessive pursuit of== ==financial parity== ==disincentivizes productivity== and overlooks the far more decisive roles played by fundamental public services and personal liberties.

To begin with, aggressively compressing the earning gap compromises economic vitality and generates widespread frustration, ultimately undermining overall social contentment. First and foremost, an artificially narrow income spectrum dampens individual ambition and entrepreneurial initiative. Human industriousness is heavily propelled by the prospect of ==meritocratic financial reward==; if individuals foresee that exceptional effort, talent, or risk-taking yield virtually the same remuneration as mediocrity, innovation inevitably stagnates and overall productivity declines. A prime historical illustration of this phenomenon can be observed in command economies of the twentieth century, where strict ==egalitarian pay structures== led to ==chronic labor apathy==, widespread shortages of consumer goods, and pervasive collective dissatisfaction. Furthermore, eliminating income differentials triggers a profound sense of injustice among highly skilled professionals. Specialists such as surgeons, aerospace engineers, and researchers endure decades of rigorous training and bear tremendous responsibilities; ==depriving them of== ==commensurate compensation== breeds resentment and induces severe ==talent flight==. For example, when high-tax jurisdictions impose ==confiscatory taxes== to equalize take-home pay, they frequently suffer an acute ==brain drain== of top medical professionals and technological innovators to more competitive nations, which inevitably degrades domestic public healthcare and cripples technological advancement for all citizens.

Equally importantly, societal happiness is fundamentally determined by the provision of comprehensive public infrastructure and intangible human rights, rather than uniform individual salaries. Primarily, ==universal access to== ==public amenities== ==serves as a far more potent buffer against== unhappiness than monetary equalization. When a government guarantees affordable housing, subsidized education, and accessible healthcare, citizens are ==insulated from== ==existential financial anxiety==, rendering differences in private luxury far less relevant to their daily peace of mind. This is well exemplified by the Nordic nations, which consistently lead global happiness rankings not because all residents earn identical wages, but because ==robust social safety nets== eliminate the dread of medical bankruptcy and educational deprivation. In addition, cultural and political factors such as personal freedom, clean governance, and environmental quality exert a profound influence on mental well-being that income parity cannot replicate. Human fulfillment hinges on intangible values like ==civic trust and personal liberties==; a society with identical wages but rampant corruption or severe environmental degradation remains inherently miserable. For instance, surveys consistently demonstrate that citizens living in relatively equal yet politically repressive or heavily polluted regions report markedly lower subjective well-being than those in open, transparent societies where income disparities may be wider but individual rights and clean air are safeguarded.

In conclusion, while moderating severe poverty is essential for social stability, guaranteeing nearly equal incomes ==is by no means the ultimate recipe for== a thriving, contented populace. Genuine societal happiness ==is best cultivated by== rewarding merit and industriousness ==while== establishing equitable public infrastructure and upholding democratic freedoms that ensure every citizen can lead a dignified and secure life.
