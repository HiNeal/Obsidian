# 动态图

## Introduction

> 🎯 **功能**：改写题目（说明什么图、比什么指标、在哪比、时间多长）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`The [图表类型] depicts [核心指标] across [研究对象] over [时间跨度].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`The line graph depicts the amounts of CO₂ released per capita across four countries (the United Kingdom, Sweden, Italy, and Portugal) over 40 years.`

## Overview

> 🎯 **功能**：概括总趋势（谁在涨、谁在跌）以及谁全程最高/最低。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Overall, [上升组] experienced a steady rise in [核心指标] from [起始年] to [终止年], while [下降组] witnessed a drop in the past [时间跨度]. Despite their respective increase and decrease, [核心指标] in [最高主体] and [最低主体] remained the highest and lowest of the [研究对象] throughout the period.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Overall, Portugal and Italy experienced a steady rise in average carbon dioxide emissions per person from 1967 to 2007, while the United Kingdom and Sweden witnessed a drop in the past 40 years. Despite their respective increase and decrease, the CO₂ release per capita in the United Kingdom and Portugal remained the highest and lowest of the four countries throughout the period.`

## Detail 1

> 🎯 **功能**：写上升组（交代起点、涨到多少、谁追平或反超了谁）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`In [起始年], [研究对象 A] [起点动词与数据], ranking the lowest among the [比较范围]. The figure then saw a steady rise in the following [时间跨度] to [终点数据] in [终止年], equaling the amount for [研究对象 C]. Having experienced a parallel upward trend in the past [时间跨度], [研究对象 B] reached [峰值数据] in [峰值年], and the figure plateaued in the following [时间跨度], ranking [终局排名] among the [比较范围] by the end of the period shown.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`In 1967, each person in Portugal released fewer than 2 metric tonnes of CO₂, ranking the lowest among the four countries. The figure then saw a steady rise in the following 40 years to over 5 metric tonnes in 2007, equaling the amount for Sweden. Having experienced a parallel upward trend in the past 40 years, the quantity per person in Italy reached nearly 8 metric tonnes of CO₂ in 1997, and the figure plateaued in the following 10 years, ranking the second among the four countries by the end of the period shown.`

## Detail 2

> 🎯 **功能**：写下降组（交代起点、跌到多少、谁被反超以及终点极值）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`[研究对象 C] emitted an amount of [核心指标] over [起点数据] in [起始年]. It rose in the following [时间跨度] to a peak of approximately [峰值数据] and began to experience a considerable decrease in the subsequent [时间跨度], being overtaken by [研究对象 B] in around [年份]. [研究对象 D], the group with the most significant figure in [起始年], saw a steady drop in [核心指标] in the following [时间跨度], and the figure declined from [起点数据] to [终点数据]. Despite this decline, however, [核心指标] in [研究对象 D] remained the largest in [终止年], compared to its counterparts in the other [其余对象范围].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Each Swede emitted an amount of CO₂ over 8 metric tonnes in 1967. It rose in the following 10 years to a peak of approximately 10 metric tonnes and began to experience a considerable decrease in the subsequent 30 years, being overtaken by Italy in around 1990. The United Kingdom, the country with the most significant figure in 1967, saw a steady drop in individual CO₂ emissions in the following 40 years, and the figure declined from nearly 11 to approximately 8.7. Despite this decline, however, the CO₂ emissions each Briton contributed to remained the largest in 2007, compared to its counterparts in the other three countries.`

# 静态图

## Introduction

> 🎯 **功能**：改写题目（说明什么图、比什么项目、包含哪些分组）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`The [provided bar chart / table] depicts [核心指标] across [研究对象] in the [调查周期].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`The provided bar chart depicts both residents' purchasing and drinking habits regarding coffee and tea across five Australian cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide, and Hobart) in the previous four weeks.`

## Overview

> 🎯 **功能**：概括全局（大家最喜欢选什么、最不常选什么、有没有特例）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Overall, [首选项] took the dominant position across the majority of [范围], with [例外对象] being the sole exception. In contrast, [垫底项] consistently ranked the lowest across all [全部范围], while [基准对象] [基准水位].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Overall, going to a cafe took the dominant position across the majority of cities, with Adelaide being the sole exception. In contrast, buying fresh coffee consistently ranked the lowest across all five locations, while at least half of the population in each city maintained one of these habits.`

## Detail 1

> 🎯 **功能**：写主要分组（对比第一名有多高、第二名差多少、最后一名的数据）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`In [对象 1] and [对象 2], the majority of people regarded [首选项] as [选择偏好], with the corresponding figures being [数据 1] in [对象 1] and [数据 2] in [对象 2]. A more significant proportion of residents in [对象 2] ([数据 2]) [选择动词] [次选项], compared to the percentage of those in [对象 1] showing a similar preference ([数据 1]). In contrast, those who preferred [末选项] constituted a slightly larger part of [对象 1]'s residents ([数据 1]) than the percentage in [对象 2] ([数据 2]).`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`In Sydney and Melbourne, the majority of people regarded cafes as the best choice for coffee/tea consumption, with the corresponding figures being 61% in Sydney and 63% in Melbourne. A more significant proportion of residents in Melbourne (47%) chose to buy instant coffee, compared to the percentage of those in Sydney showing a similar preference (45.2%). In contrast, those who preferred to buy fresh coffee constituted a slightly larger part of Sydney's residents (44%) than the percentage in Melbourne (42%).`

## Detail 2

> 🎯 **功能**：写其余分组（对比普遍规律、讲特例反常、总结相同或接近的数据）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`[首选项] remained people's priority for [主题] in the remaining [数量] [范围], except in [例外对象], where people showed marginally more interest in [次选项] ([数据 A]) rather than [首选项] ([数据 B]). In [对象 3] and [对象 4], the percentages of residents who [行为倾向] were almost equivalent, both [持平门槛], [差值] lower than a similarly low [对象 5]. People in the [数量] cities who favored [项目] occupied at least half of the whole population, with respective figures being [数据 3] ([对象 3]), [数据 4] ([对象 4]), and [数据 5] ([对象 5]).`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`A cafe remained people's priority for coffee/tea in the remaining three cities, except in Adelaide, where people showed marginally more interest in buying instant coffee (50%) rather than going to a cafe (49.5%). In Brisbane and Adelaide, the percentages of residents who were inclined to buy fresh coffee were almost equivalent, both under 35%, 3% lower than a similarly low Hobart. People in the three cities who favored instant coffee occupied at least half of the whole population, with respective figures being 53% (Brisbane), 50% (Adelaide), and 54% (Hobart).`

# 流程图

## Introduction

> 🎯 **功能**：改写题目（说明这是什么工艺或产品的制作/回收流程）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`The [flow diagram / process chart] illustrates how [核心对象] is/are [核心工序动词].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`The flow diagram illustrates how plastic bottles are recycled.`

## Overview

> 🎯 **功能**：概括全局（一共分几步、从最初原料到最终产出的简述）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Overall, there are [数量] distinct steps in [工艺主题]. Roughly speaking, [原料/物料] are [工序动词 1], [工序动词 2], [工序动词 3], and finally made into [终端产物].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Overall, there are 9 distinct steps in plastic-bottle recycling. Roughly speaking, plastic bottles are collected, sorted, broken down, and finally made into new products.`

## Detail 1

> 🎯 **功能**：写前半段工序（从原料收集、运输、分拣到初步处理）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`The process begins with the collection of [初始原料] from [来源场所列举]. They are then transported by [运输方式] to [处理场所] for the further [工序目的] process. At the [处理场所], [物料] are manually sorted and separated from [杂质/无关物], with [可用物料] set apart for the following procedure. Those [物料] are subsequently compressed into blocks and then crushed into [形态产物], which are later washed in a container to remove all residues.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`The process begins with the collection of used plastic bottles from homes, businesses, and other sites. They are then transported by truck to the recycling center for the further sorting process. At the center, plastic bottles are manually sorted and separated from irrelevant items, with recyclable bottles set apart for the following procedure. Those bottles are subsequently compressed into blocks and then crushed into small pieces, which are later washed in a container to remove all residues.`

## Detail 2

> 🎯 **功能**：写后半段工序（从深度加工、模具成型到成品入市使用）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Once cleaned, [中间物料] are then sent into [设备名称] to produce [中间半成品], and those [半成品] are heated afterward so that they become the raw materials for new products. Finally, end products are molded in various forms: [成品类别列举], and others. Those products then enter the market to fulfill their missions in [应用场景], potentially to be recycled someday in the future.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Once cleaned, those fragments are then sent into a machine to produce plastic pellets, and those pellets are heated afterward so that they become the raw materials for new products. Finally, end products are molded in various forms: plastic bottles, pencils, shirts, bags, and others. Those products then enter the market to fulfill their missions in people's daily lives, potentially to be recycled someday in the future.`

# 地图

## Introduction

> 🎯 **功能**：改写题目（说明是哪里的地图、对比过去与现在或未来规划）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`The diagrams show [现状布局] and illustrate [规划变迁] for [未来时间].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`The diagrams show the current layout of the Southwest Airport and illustrate some proposed changes to it for the forthcoming year.`

## Overview

> 🎯 **功能**：概括全局（最核心的大扩建/大改造是什么、配套设施有哪些变化）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Overall, it is clear that the principal change is the extension of [核心区域], giving rise to [扩容增量结果]. Various other developments with regard to [配套领域 A] and [配套领域 B] are also included in the proposed plan.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Overall, it is clear that the principal change is the extension of the gate area, giving rise to an additional number of gates. Various other developments with regard to shops and passenger services are also included in the proposed plan.`

## Detail 1

> 🎯 **功能**：写核心区域变迁（具体怎么扩建、增加了哪些关键大设施）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`Looking at the current layout, we can see that [主体设施] has [初始规模数量] with a [通道设施] transporting people to [连接区域]. The new design proposal, however, involves an extension of [扩建区域] to [倍数规模], thus allowing [新增设施数量] and [新增配套设施] to be included. Before entering [新区域], [使用人群] will also be able to [功能动作] in [拟建配套设施] to be built.`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`Looking at the current layout, we can see that the airport has 8 gates with a walkway transporting people to different gates. The new design proposal, however, involves an extension of the current gate space to the two times bigger size, thus allowing 10 more gates and a sky train to be included. Before entering the newly designed gate area, passengers will also be able to shop in several stores to be built.`

## Detail 2

> 🎯 **功能**：写周边配套变迁（哪些地方没变、旧设施搬去哪、新添了哪些小设施）。
> 
> 🦴 **骨架**：`While [未变区域 A] and [未变区域 B] will remain their original size, [新增设施] will come into operation for [服务目的]. In [特定区域], [原设施 1] and [原设施 2] will be relocated so that a [新功能设施] can be brought into operation. The [次要/闲置区域], whose space is currently idle, will see the proposed construction of [设施 1], [设施 2], and [设施 3].`
> 
> 📝 **范例**：`While the departure and arrival areas will remain their original size, more shops will come into operation for customers' convenience. In the departure area, the check-in counter and cafe will be relocated so that a bag-drop can be brought into operation. The arrival area, whose space is currently idle, will see the proposed construction of a cafe, an ATM, and a site for hiring cars.`
