# 原文

(A) The ==Industrial Revolution== (1760–1850) began in England and spread throughout Europe and the Americas ==over the course== of the next several decades. The Industrial Revolution did not reach America until the 1820s and began with the textile industries in the Northeast. Expanding technology and the invention of new machines forever changed the way manufacturing and industry took place. The textile industry was greatly impacted by a number of new inventions such as the flying ==shuttle==, the ==spinning== ==frame== and the ==cotton== ==gin==. But it was the invention of the Spinning Jenny by James Hargreaves that ==is credited with== moving the textile industry from homes to factories. The move from a domestic, ==cottage-based== industry to factories allowed the expansion of the Industrial Revolution from England ==throughout== much of the world.

(B) James Hargreaves was a ==weaver== in the village of Stanhill, near Blackburn, Lancashire. Prior to the Industrial Revolution, farming was the main ==occupation== in the area, while the production of cloth from ==raw goods== was carried out as a secondary activity within cottage industries. All work was done by individuals within the home, and entire families were involved. Men were often the weavers, while their wives and daughters spun the raw materials into threads or yarns. Young children assisted in cleaning and preparing the fibres. The process was time-consuming, and merchants wanting to meet the demand for textile goods ==were often frustrated by== the huge gap between ==supply and demand==.

(C) The expense involved in transporting raw materials to numerous locations, waiting for finished textiles to be made by hand, and then the finished goods to be picked up and transported back to the merchant was also an ==incentive== to search for alternatives. While the invention of the flying ==shuttle== and later the power ==loom== made it possible to weave materials faster, the spinning of raw materials into ==threads== and ==yarns== was still done by hand, one thread at a time. Spinners were unable to keep up with the increased demand for threads.

(D) James Hargreaves may have designed the spinning jenny as an improvement over an earlier machine invented by Thomas High in 1763 or 1764. While never ==patented==, High’s machine used six spinning wheels that were ==bolted== together and powered by a single large wheel. James Hargreaves’ spinning jenny was said to have been designed after he saw his wife’s spinning wheel ==knocked over== and saw the spindle continue to spin. The spinning jenny used eight different ==spindles== that were powered by a single wheel. This allowed one spinner to produce eight threads in the same amount of time that it previously took to produce one.

(E) The idea was developed by Hargreaves as a metal frame with eight wooden spindles at one end. A set of eight ==rovings== was attached to a ==beam== on that frame. The rovings when extended passed through two horizontal bars of wood that could be ==clasped== together. These bars could be drawn along the top of the frame by the spinner’s left hand, thus extending the thread. The spinner used his right hand to rapidly turn a wheel, which caused all the spindles to ==revolve==, and the thread to be ==spun==. When the bars were returned, the thread wound onto the spindle. A pressing ==wire==, known as a ==faller==, was used to guide the threads onto the right place on the spindle. The rollers were linked to the hand wheel by a cord, which transferred the ==motion== throughout the machine.

(F) Later versions of the spinning jenny added even more spindles, which made the machine too large for home use. This led the way to factories where these larger machines could be run by fewer workers and operated using new sources of power, such as waterwheels and later steam engines. The shift from home-based production to ==centralized== factory systems transformed the textile industry, allowing manufacturers to produce yarn in far greater quantities and at significantly lower cost. As factory owners invested in additional machinery, productivity increased rapidly, creating a model of industrial manufacturing that would soon spread across Britain and throughout the world. The spinning jenny therefore played a ==crucial== role not only in increasing output but also in laying the foundation for large-scale ==industrialisation==.

# 错题记录

Which invention made it possible to weave materials faster? \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: flying shuttle (或 the flying shuttle)
> 错误: wheel
> 常见陷阱: 细节错配
> 直接原因: C 段明确写 While the invention of the flying shuttle and later the power loom made it possible to weave materials faster, 加快织布速度的发明是 flying shuttle; wheel 在 D、E 段是用于纺纱(spinning)的轮子, 与题干 weave materials faster 对应不上。
> 破局法则: 核对三点: 先锁定题干动作 weave materials faster, 回原文 C 段区分织布(weave)与纺纱(spin), 再取发明名称 flying shuttle。
