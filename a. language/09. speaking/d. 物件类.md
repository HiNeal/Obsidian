# Part2 物件类

> 全部文章为讲义逐字原文; **加粗部分 = 母版语言**, 正常字体 = 结合题目新写的部分

## 物件类的题目范围

有具体的（一个蛋糕、一首歌）, 也有抽象的（一份礼物、离不开的东西、传统物品、一项发明）。看似"蛋糕题"和"歌曲题"互斥要写两篇——其实**一篇文章串起所有物件题**。

## 母版：特别的蛋糕

Describe an occasion when you had a cake that was special.

This reminds me of a cake made by my best friend. I think it was my 19th birthday. My best friend was being mysterious all day. She told me she had a big surprise for me. We met at a nice little restaurant as planned. She carefully placed a beautiful cake on the table. It was a handmade mousse cake, my favorite flavor! She told me, she spent the whole day making it. I felt so excited, so happy, so, how to say, exhilarated to receive such a present.

She was not a professional chef. But this is a unique present. One of a kind! Think about it, she had to search for the recipe, collect all the ingredients, and all the mixing and stirring! After all, she could just buy one, right? But she did not. That made the cake so special and so precious.

And I still remember the end of that day, we talked a lot, from the cake to other stuff, it seems that there used to be a line, I mean, a boundary, between us, but because of the cake, the boundary was gone. We became much closer, and that's great.

When you think about friendship, or any other interpersonal relationships, it is always about loving and caring for each other. I mean, no one is an island. Sending a handmade gift is the best way to say: we are bonding. When someone receives something so kind, so personal, so genuine, they will be touched.

### 母版的设计深意 ⭐

- 全文**只有一句在讲怎么做蛋糕**——太具体的内容会锁死文章, 难套其他题
- 大量篇幅给了**人情世故**（聊天、隔阂消失、变亲密、no one is an island）——**人情世故的内容普适性最强, 最容易套进任何题目**。自己写文章也应从人情世故多着笔墨

### 想送朋友的礼物

Describe a gift you would like to buy for a friend.——**注意时态**：收蛋糕是过去时, "想送"还没发生, 用现在/将来时; 模板身份反转（我来做蛋糕）。

Well, I think I won't buy anything for my friend. I mean, we are very close; we have known each other for 10 years, so I would choose something more meaningful: maybe a handmade gift.

A handmade mousse cake seems to be a nice choice. I **am not a professional chef. But this is** going to be **a unique present. One of a kind!** I know it's not easy to make a cake. I need to **search for the recipe, collect all the ingredients, and all the mixing and stirring!** But I also know that my friend will be **so excited, so happy, so, how to say, exhilarated to receive such a present.**

And I still remember the last time I had given a DIY gift to my **friend, we talked a lot, from the** gift **to other stuff, it seems that there used to be a line, I mean, a boundary, between us, but because of the** gift, **the boundary was gone. We became much closer, and that's great.**

A DIY gift has a lot of layers of meaning. **Because when you think about friendship, or any other interpersonal relationships, it is always about loving and caring for each other. I mean, no one is an island. Sending a handmade gift is the best way to say: we are bonding. When someone receives something so kind, so personal, so genuine, they will be touched.**

| 表达                                       | 释义                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| close / have known each other for 10 years | 照应指令"你们多熟/认识多久" |
| a lot of layers of meaning                 | 多层含义（高级表达）        |

男生觉得蛋糕偏女性化？换任何**亲手做的东西**都行。

| 表达                    | 释义                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| striped                 | 条纹的                                                             |

Looking back, I still find **it a very unique present. One of a kind! Think about it,** my aunt made it all by herself! **She had to** prepare all the materials, design the look, and all the other **stuff. After all, she could just buy one, right? But she did not. That made the** toy **so special and so precious.**

And I still remember last year, my aunt and I talked about all the memories. **We talked a lot,** from the bear **to other stuff, it seems that there used to be a line, I mean, a boundary, between** us, but because of the bear, **the boundary was gone. We became much closer, and that's great.**

**When you think about interpersonal relationships, it is always about loving and caring for each other. I mean, no one is an island. Sending a handmade gift is the best way to say: we are bonding. When someone receives something so kind, so personal, so genuine, they will be touched.**

### 离不开的东西 ⭐ 难题

Describe something you can't live without — not a mobile/cell phone or computer.

A mobile phone or a computer is of course very important, we can't live without them. But besides them... if I have to say... perhaps we need something more meaningful here, something that we are attached to, I guess. For me, actually, it's a song.

It's a song written **by my best friend. I think it was my 19th birthday. She gave me a DVD and told me she had recorded a song on it. She gave it to me as a birthday present. I felt so excited, so happy, so, how to say, exhilarated to receive such a present.** And from then on, I couldn't live without it; especially when she was not nearby.

The song was an interesting variation on the common "happy birthday song". Every year, I will listen to it. It reminds me of **how she had to create the tune, write all the lyrics, and all the recording and editing.**

The song is totally different from a mobile phone, or a computer, of course. A mobile is more functional, so we can't live without it; but for me, the song is more personal, more emotional, it **says a lot about friendship, or any other interpersonal relationships, it is always about loving and caring for each other. Sending a handmade gift is the best way to say: we are bonding. When someone receives something so kind, so personal, so genuine, they will be touched.**

| 表达                | 释义                                           |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| be attached to      | 依恋、离不开                                   |
| functional          | 实用的（与"情感的"相对——手机实用 vs 歌曲情感） |
| personal, emotional | 私人的、情感层面的                             |

要点：①送歌人必须设定成**亲密的人**（挚友/亲人/恋人）, 否则"离不开一首歌"不成立; ②全文反复强调"离不开"（couldn't live without it / every year I will listen to it）; ③老师口头补充可加的结尾：每次换新手机都会把这首歌导进去, it has become an emotional support。

### 国家的传统物品

Describe a traditional product made in your country which you like.——别去查瓷器、刺绣、剪纸（为一道题查资料不划算）, **编一个"护身符"**。

We Chinese have that kind of little thing, made by ourselves, and can protect us... how to say... like an amulet? That's very traditional, and almost everyone likes it. My mother made one for me.

**I think it was my 19th birthday.** My mother **was being mysterious all day. She told me she had a big surprise for me. We met at a nice little restaurant as planned. She gave me** a beautiful bracelet made of string. **She told me, she spent the whole day making it. I felt so excited, so happy, so, how to say, exhilarated to receive such a present.**

**She was not a professional** handicraftsman. **But this is a unique present. One of a kind! Think about it, she had to** search for the string, design the look, and all the other stuff! **After all, she could just buy one, right? But she did not. That made the present so special and so precious.**

**And I still remember the end of that day, we talked a lot, from the** bracelet **to other stuff, it seems that there used to be a line, I mean, a boundary, between us, but because of the** bracelet, **the boundary was gone. We became much closer, and that's great.**

I think this is the point of giving handmade amulet. It's for protection, of course, but it also represents sincere relationship. **When you think about relationship** between parents and kids, **or any other interpersonal relationships, it is always about loving and caring for each other. I mean, no one is an island. Sending a handmade** amulet **is the best way to say: we are bonding. When someone receives something so kind, so personal, so genuine, they will be touched.**

| 表达                    | 释义     |
| ----------------------- | -------- |
| amulet                  | 护身符   |
| bracelet made of string | 绳编手镯 |
| handicraftsman          | 手工艺人 |
| represent               | 代表     |

**⭐ 重要语音技巧——难词前要"犹豫"**：说 amulet 这种高大上的词, 千万不要一上来自信流畅直接说出（一听就是背稿）。要像原文那样**用一长串简单、充满不确定的话铺垫**（that kind of little thing... how to say... like...）, 最后才把难词吐出来。另外：**考官完全不会质疑"护身符"是否真实存在**——考察的是表达, 不是内容的真实性。

### 帮助你专注的东西

Describe something that helps you to concentrate on work/study.——把 something 解读成**一个方法**（抽象化）, 跨类别反套聪明学长。

Recently, I've learned a method, it's very effective and efficient, it helps me to concentrate on my study, especially on my IELTS studying. That is, to set a small goal in a short period of time.

Actually, I learned this method from an influencer. **His name is CongMingXueZhang, which can be loosely translated as Mr. Smart. He is an influencer on DouYin, the Chinese version of TikTok. He creates videos about his studying methods and experience.** I learned a lot from him, it helps me to concentrate on my study.

In his first video, he showed how to memorize 10 difficult words in 10 minutes. That was a small goal, and you only need to concentrate for 10 minutes, it's quite easy. This video received a lot of likes. **It was so interesting, so intriguing, and it captivated me at once, and I** started following him since then.

And last week, I used this method to challenge myself. **I managed to remember 1000 words in three days. I mean, come on, that's more than difficult, it's very difficult, it's a daunting task. But, I did it!** 1000 words is a big goal, but I made some changes. **I divided these words into different groups, and adopted various ways to memorize them, and even took advantage of different locations to consolidate the memories.** Actually, every day I only spent 4 hours, it was not tiring, and I could easily focus, and finally, I nailed all the words. **At last, I was surprised, no, I was more than surprised, I was stunned.**

So you see, once we have very big goals, there may be some distraction; but if we divide the goal into smaller ones, it's easier for us to concentrate. This is what I learned from Mr. Smart.

| 表达                   | 释义                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| effective / efficient  | 有效的 / 高效的——**高效 ⊇ 有效**（有效且效率高才叫高效）, 写作常用、极易混淆 |
| receive a lot of likes | 收获很多赞                                                                   |
| nail                   | 搞定（很口语的说法）                                                         |
| distraction            | 让人分心的事物（concentrate 的反义呼应, 主题词）                             |

注意人称转换：模板里"他"三天背千词, 这里改成**我**用他的方法挑战自己。

### 改变世界的发明 ⭐ 脑洞题

Describe an invention which you think has changed the world in a good way.——别写火车飞机手机互联网（要另写一篇）, 写 **VR, 反套地点类的乡下模板**。

I have to say it's the VR technology. VR is short for... I guess... virtual reality, maybe? I am not sure about the term, but I am quite certain it has changed, and will go on to change the world.

With a pair of VR glasses, you can go anywhere you like. For example, **I have always been very attached to the countryside. My grandparents live there and I went there every summer. It is just so peaceful and quiet, or you may say, it is tranquil. It slows everything down, and blocks out all the hustle and bustle of the city.**

Basically, I can go there once a year, but with the help of the VR glasses, I can go there, and anywhere I like. I bought a VR service, it gives me access to different natural sights in China. With the glasses on, **it is easy for me to feel the beauty of nature,** as if I were there. **I can see the little buds and the sprouting flowers. I mean, you can see the change of seasons, and it's with your own eyes. Everything can be so natural, so real, and so tangible.**

And more amazingly, VR is called virtual reality, and it helps me to escape from the reality I hate. **I mean, people living in the city are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can feel the distance between people.** But with the VR glasses on, I can escape from the city and fly all the way to the countryside. **In the countryside, things are totally different. The community there is small but close-knit. People know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other. It's good to know there is always a place for you.** Even though you know, this place is not real.

Maybe one day, with the help of VR, I will not be sitting here taking the exam, I will be at home, with glasses on, and the test would be done.

| 表达                                                  | 释义                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| VR is short for... I guess... virtual reality, maybe? | 高难缩写要"犹豫"着说, 别流畅背出 |
| term                                                  | 术语                             |
| give sb access to                                     | 让某人得以使用/解锁……的权限      |
| as if I were there                                    | 虚拟语气——毕竟不是真的在那里     |
| escape from the reality                               | 虚拟现实 vs 逃离现实的文字游戏   |
| Even though this place is not real                    | 结尾呼应 VR 的"虚拟"属性         |
