# Part2 地点类

> 方法论与人物类完全一致; 全部文章为讲义逐字原文, **加粗部分 = 母版语言**, 正常字体 = 结合题目新写的部分

## 地点类的题目范围

乡下、房子/公寓、新的地方、城市、国家、咖啡馆、图书馆……**不论怎么变都是就地点发问**, 可以用一个故事串起所有题目。老师选母版时把所有地点题看了一遍, 专挑**能覆盖最多题目**的例子（乡下）, 并在母版里用隐晦的方式呼应多个题目。

## 母版：乡下（爷爷奶奶家）

Describe a place in the countryside that you enjoyed visiting.

8分水平约250词（范文按8分写, 删掉难词难句后仍有6.5~7分）。三大内容支柱：**宁静**（tranquil）→ **自然之美**（亲眼看到四季）→ **人情味**（bond）。

Well, I have always been very attached to the countryside. My grandparents live there and I went there last summer. It is just so peaceful and quiet, or you may say, it is tranquil. It slows everything down, and blocks out all the hustle and bustle of the city.

I go to the countryside every summer. It is easier for me to feel the beauty of nature when I am there. You can always find peace there. It's not a cliché, I really feel so. I do. In the countryside, you don't need someone else to tell you spring is here. Instead, you get to see the little buds and the sprouting flowers. I mean, you can see the change of seasons, and it's with your own eyes. Everything can be so natural, so real, and so tangible.

Last summer, when I was in the countryside, I found something even more meaningful: the bonds between people. I mean, people living in the city are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can feel the distance between people. We are separated by endless traffic jams, by all the tall, fancy but boring buildings. But when I was in the countryside, things were totally different. The community there is small but close-knit. People know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other. It's good to know there is always a place for you.

### 母版中的套路句式

| 句式                                               | 原句                                                  | 说明                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| It is just so A and B, **or you may say**, it is C | so peaceful and quiet, or you may say, it is tranquil | 逐渐高级化：先简单词 A、B, 再用 or you may say 引出高级词 C |
| It's not a cliché, I really feel so. I do.         | 同左                                                  | 把可能显俗的观点说得真诚                                    |
| so A, so B, and so C                               | so natural, so real, and so tangible                  | 平行结构, **C 位放最亮眼的词**                              |
| always so A, always B-ing                          | always so busy, always buzzing around                 | 平行+同义替换                                               |
| A each other, B each other, and C each other       | know, care about, sympathize with                     | 平行三连, C 最高级                                          |

平行结构 ABC 通用规律：**C 通常最高级、放最后**; A、B 简单省脑细胞; 三者近义或递进, 逻辑紧密所以好背。

### 换例子：成都（城市版）

He/She 不变题目变例子的示范——同理可改咖啡馆（第二段谈咖啡、饼干、蛋糕）、图书馆/书店（第二段谈书, 第三段谈安静中的 bond 与店外喧嚣截然不同）。

**Well, I have always been very attached to** a modern city like Chengdu. **My grandparents live there and I went there last summer. It is just so** modern and fashionable, **or you may say, it is** cosmopolitan. **It** makes everything special, and you want to linger once you go there, especially because of the great food.

**I go to** Chengdu **every summer. It is easier for me to feel the beauty of** food **when I am there. You can always** feed your soul **there. It's not a cliché, I really feel so. I do. In** Chengdu, **you don't need someone else to tell you** dinner is ready. **Instead, you get to see the** various food and drinks along the streets. **I mean, you can** feel your saliva dropping. **Everything can be so** yummy, **so** delicious, **and so** palatable.

**Last summer, when I was in** Chengdu, **I found something even more meaningful: the bonds between people. I mean, people living in** other big cities **are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can feel the distance between people. They are separated by endless traffic jams, by all the tall, fancy but boring buildings. But when I was in** Chengdu, **things were totally different. The community there is small but close-knit. People know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other. It's good to know there is always a place for you.**

### 去过的一个新地方

Describe a new place you visited.

I remember it was like eight years ago, I was brought by my parents to visit a distant relative. At the very beginning, I didn't want to go at all. Because the family lived in a remote village. I mean, no one likes to be stuck at such a boring place, right?

But I totally changed my mind after the visit. **It was just so peaceful and quiet, or you may say, it was tranquil. It slowed everything down, and blocked out all the hustle and bustle of the city.**

The local people in the village were very hospitable to me, even though I was a total stranger. I could feel the special **bonds between people. I mean, people living in the city are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can feel the distance between people. We are separated by endless traffic jams, by all the tall, fancy but boring buildings. But when I was in the countryside, things were totally different. The community there is small but close-knit. People know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other. It's good to know there is always a place for you.**

At that time, all of these were very new to me and now it's my unique experience.

| 表达             | 释义                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| distant relative | 远房亲戚                       |
| remote           | 偏远的                         |
| be stuck at      | 被困在（后也可接困境 dilemma） |
| hospitable       | 热情好客的                     |
| a total stranger | 素不相识的陌生人               |

注意母版时态整体改成过去时（was tranquil / slowed / blocked）。结尾一句照应题目的"新"。

### 有趣的著名城市（桂林）

Describe a famous city that you find interesting.——选桂林：山水城市天然衔接乡下母版（国际化大都市反而难套）。前两段自编（本题唯一有背诵压力的部分）, 后两段全模板。

The first city that popped into my mind is Guilin. It's there, in the textbook, that's how I knew it. It's famous for its jaw-dropping scenery. The city is like a dream destination for millions of tourists, home and abroad.

There is a very interesting rule set by the local government. That is, no buildings in the city are allowed to be higher than 25 meters. Why? It is to make sure that buildings do not stand in the way of nature.

One of the benefits is that **it is easier for** residents **to feel the beauty of nature in the city.** In Guilin, **you do not need someone else to tell you spring is here. Instead, you get to see the little buds and the sprouting flowers. I mean, you can see the change of seasons, and it's with your own eyes. Everything can be so natural, so real, and so tangible.**

Another special thing about the city is **the bonds between people.** If you think about Beijing, the capital of China, **people are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can always feel the distance between people. They are separated by endless traffic jams, by all the tall, fancy but boring buildings.** But you see, things in Guilin are very different. **The community there is small but close-knit. People know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other. It's good to know there is always a place for you.**

### 国内重要的水域 ⭐ 难题示范

Describe an important river, lake or area of water in your country.——看似完全套不上的题, **不查任何资料, 10 分钟用模板变出来**。

Actually, there is a river in my hometown called NanJiang. I am not very sure whether it's important for the country or not, I mean, in terms of economy or politics, but it's quite famous.

**I have always been very attached to** the river. My grandparents, my parents and I, we all live near the riverside. **It is just so peaceful and quiet, or you may say, it is tranquil. It slows everything down, and blocks out all the hustle and bustle of the city.**

Actually, I don't know how big or how long the river is. But when I am facing it, it seems to be really wide, really long, really spectacular. **It is easier for me to feel the beauty of nature when I am there. You can always find peace there. It's not a cliché, I really feel so. I do. You can see the change** in river flows, **and it's with your own eyes. Everything can be so natural, so real, and so tangible.**

Nowadays, a lot of people come to see the river because it's beautiful, but for me, it's just a river, a river I grew up with. Near it, I can feel **the bonds between people. I mean, people living in the city are always so busy. They are always buzzing around, never willing to spare a second to say hi. You can feel the distance between people. We are separated by endless traffic jams, by all the tall, fancy but boring buildings.** But when we are near the river, things are totally different. It seems near the water, in the wind, people begin to slow down, begin to know each other, care about each other, and sympathize with each other.

| 表达                            | 释义                                   |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| in terms of economy or politics | 关于经济和政治（层面）                 |
| riverside                       | 河边                                   |
| spectacular                     | 壮观的                                 |
| the change in river flows       | 河流波动的变化（母版"四季变化"的改造） |

两个重要观念：

- **"我不确定它重不重要"不会因偏题扣分**——雅思口语考交流, 不在乎内容是否 100% 扣题, 考察的是流利表达的能力
- 老师当年备考会去查长江黄河的资料、为一道题写一整晚全新稿子——**从大局看完全不划算**。正确姿势：用已有模板去套, 绝不为单题另起炉灶
