# Part3 深入问答

## Part3 定位

- 难度大于 Part1/2, 且**拿不到题**——即使网友回忆的真题也难保证复现
- 很多书籍课程略讲 Part3, 甚至说"不追求6分以上可以放弃"。老师反对：①无法保证前两部分发挥完美稳拿6分; ②申请 G5 以上/美加名校常需口语 6.5~7, **Part3 起决定性作用**
- 官方明确说法：**想得 6 分以上者必须重视 Part3**
- 为什么 Part3 是高分主战场：①问题更难 → 更能测出**复杂观点和复杂词汇**的掌握; ②考官知道大家都背了 Part1/2, Part3 是他自由发挥、**测你真实水平**的机会

## 考试形式

进入 Part3 前考官会说：

> We've been talking about [你的Part2话题], and I'd like to discuss with you one or two more general questions related to this. So let's consider, first of all...

| 要点       | 说明                                                                                                                                |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 话题       | 顺着 Part2 深入, 更 general、更宽泛抽象、与社会现实紧密结合, 像大作文                                                               |
| 题量       | 官方说 1-2 个, 实际 **3~6 个**; 总时长约 5 分钟                                                                                     |
| 无准备时间 | 考官问完立刻答（Part2 还有 1 分钟, Part3 没有）                                                                                     |
| 数量博弈   | 回答越长, 考官能问的越少。答满 2 分钟（200~250词）≈ 只被问 2 题; 只答 1 分钟（100~150词）会被问 3-4 题 → **say as much as you can** |
| 问法固定   | 具体题目千变万化, 但 Who / How important / In what ways / Why / Should 这些**问法反复出现** —— 这就是突破口                         |

## 备考策略

| 目标      | 策略                 |
| --------- | -------------------- |
| 追求 6 分 | Part3 **少丢分**     |
| 6 分以上  | Part3 **尽量拿高分** |

三层准备：

1. **能准备的题**（老师从几百道考生回忆题中甄选的高频题）→ 准备充分, 甚至逐字稿背诵
2. **不能准备的题** → 靠下面的【经典思维套路 + 语言套话】：撑字数、展示词汇量、**给大脑争取思考缓冲时间**（前提：背得滚瓜烂熟）
3. 剩下约 **50% 靠临场发挥**——"学长只能送你到这了"

适用人群：目标 6 分/四六级 400 左右 → 非常适合; 四六级 600+、追求 8 分 → 可以只参考思路, 用自己的话 paraphrase, 模板反而可能限制你。

## 七大常见问法：思维套路 + 语言模板 ⭐

> 总结自过去三四百道 Part3 真题, 覆盖 80% 以上的题目, 每个模板能替你填充约一半的回答时间。省略号处**都设计成只需填一个完整句子**——加整句不会破坏模板的语法正确性。

### 问法1：请罗列出 XX

（Who do people... / What kinds of things... / What are the most popular types of...）

思维套路：确实有不同种类 → 这事挺复杂, 一一列举有难度 → 但我可以根据周围和中国的情况分析一下, 至少举两个例子 → 第一…第二… → 现实更复杂, 但这两个例子应该还行。

> Oh, yeah, it is true that + 题目同义改写.
> I guess this issue is kind of complicated; if I have to create different categories for that, it might be kind of challenging.
> But based on what I have seen around me, and the situation in China, I guess I can list two of them.
> Firstly, ......
> Secondly, ......
> I know the reality is actually far more complicated, but as far as I know, these two types may be two good examples.

（模板约 90 词——中等语速已够撑 1 分钟）

- 同义改写和写作不同：**稍微照应题目即可**, 不必逐字改写
- 模板三大好处：①背熟后脱口而出不卡壳 ②语言零错误还带亮眼词（categories/challenging）③说模板的同时**大脑飞速运转想具体例子**

### 问法2：某事对不同人群的区别

（old people / children / boys / girls / men / women...）

思维套路：确实应该分年龄/性别讨论 → 我不能代表所有人, 先从我代表的人群说起 → 如果是年轻人的话……（发挥）→ 老人的话情况可能不同, 我不太确定, 但想象一下的话我觉得…… → 这差不多就是我的想法：年轻人我比较确定, 但同样情况不一定适用于老年人。

> Indeed, I think things are totally different for people of different ages/genders.
> Since I myself am young/a man/a woman, I will first talk about someone like me, what the situation is about them.
> For young people/men/women, I guess it's like ......
> But when it comes to someone who is old, things might be different. I'm not pretty sure, but if we are allowed to imagine here, I'd like to say that maybe ......
> This is actually what I think about this issue. While I'm pretty sure about the situation for someone like me, young people mainly, it's hard to say that the same situation applies to the old people as well.

（约 100 词; **apply to**——A 的情况也适用于 B, 亮眼短语）

### 问法3：过去、现在和将来

（in the past / in the future / now and in the past...）

思维套路：还真没考虑过这个问题 → 过去、现在、未来情况还真有点不一样 → 先说现在（最了解）→ 过去 20 年（**带入自己的年纪**）好像是…… → 更久远我不清楚, 但听家人提起过, 我妈说…… → 预测未来不确定, 但感觉上…… → 差不多就是这样。

> Actually, I really gave some thought to questions like this.（give thought to 思考过）
> I kind of have the feeling that things might be different in the past, in the current way, and in the future as well.
> Firstly, nowadays. Because it's the current situation, I know it very well. It's like ......（it's like 委婉表示"不是百分百确定"）
> Secondly, if I look back, like, in the past 20 years I have been through, it was like ......（注意句子改用过去时）
> But if we are talking about something in a more remote past, I am not sure, but I have heard from my family members. My mother actually said ......
> Finally, if I am to predict about the future, I am not sure, but I have the feeling that ......
> Ok, I think that's all for my opinions.

（约 125 词）

⭐ 题目只问其中一个维度（只问过去、只问未来）也可以三段全说——**官方指南认可**：考察的是口语能力, 不是回答与题目多么滴水不漏。

### 问法4：利与弊

（benefits / drawbacks / advantages / problems...）

思维套路：这种事确实可能有比较复杂的影响 → 分类的话我觉得利大于弊（或弊大于利）→ 第一个想到的利…… → 另外不能忽略的利…… → 当然也有弊, 第一个想到的是…… → 还有人说有这样的弊, 我也挺同意 → 所以这事有利有弊。

> It is true that things like this may have various impacts.
> If we are going to divide these impacts into positive and negative ones, I would say that the benefits are more/fewer than the negative influence.
> Firstly, I think it's beneficial because ......
> Also, I guess we should also notice that ......
> On the other hand, there might be some drawbacks as well. The first that comes to my mind is that ......
> Also, a lot of people say that ......, and I agree.
> So, to sum up, it has both positive and negative aspects.

（约 100 词; 模板已内置同义多样化：impacts/influence、positive/beneficial、negative/drawbacks）

- 利、弊各举两个; 来不及就各举一个
- 说模板时大脑可同步用写作课的**换对象法**找要点：对家长/对学生/对学校……往往一两秒就能想到

### 问法5：请给出原因

（reasons / why...）

思维套路：为什么呀？我想一下。确实是这样（改写题目）→ 深究背后原因有几个影响因素 → 首先和社会整体有关：现在社会上很多人都在做这事, 其他人可能只是有样学样、跟随主流——也就是**羊群效应** → 另外确实和个人喜好有关：每人看法、价值观不一样, 所以这很正常 → 再找其他原因我不太确定, 但可能和经济/科技/政治有关 → 总的来说原因不少, 但最重要的还是我说的这两三个。

> Why? ...... Let me think. ...... It is true that ......（同义改写题目）
> But if we are going to delve into the reasons behind it（delve into 深入研究）, I guess some factors might be responsible.
> Firstly, I guess it has something to do with the society as a whole. I mean, a lot of people nowadays actually ......, so other people might be just following their lead（有样学样）, or, how to say, to comply with the standard, or, you know, the Herd Behavior Effect.（羊群效应）
> But, of course, I guess it also has something to do with individual preferences. I mean, different people have different views, different values, so it's very common that some people ......
> Finally, if we are looking for more reasons, it may have something to do with, like, economy, technology, politics, I guess, but I'm not sure.
> There are a lot of reasons behind this phenomenon, but I guess the ones I just mentioned are the most important two or three reasons.

（约 150 词）

- **羊群效应几乎是万能真实原因**：Part3 问原因的题大多问"社会普遍现象为什么", 答"人们随大流"逻辑自洽——这已经不算套话而是真原因
- how to say 引出 comply with the standard; you know 接羊群效应（稍有文化的人都听过, 用法正确）

### 问法6：谁应该怎么做

（Should parents... / How can government/schools/managers...）

思维套路：的确这事特别值得做 → 但要给出具体建议有点难 → **我们是否应该先弄清问题的原因, 再想对策呢？**（老师自评"鸡贼", 但确实贴题：谈对策先谈成因）→ 比如这个问题很明显, 其成因是…… → 知道了原因, 对策就简单多了：XX 应该…… → 另外我们还可以……

> I guess no one will deny that it's important that ......（同义改写）
> But if we are going to come up with some specific proposals（proposal 建议）, I think it requires more analysis.
> I guess perhaps we need to figure out what the current problem is, and then we can have a solution.
> For example, it's obvious that ......（问题）and the reason behind it, I guess, is that ......（原因）
> So, now we know the cause, then we can have a solution. Firstly, it is important that ...(should)...（注意 it is important that 后的虚拟语气）
> Also, maybe it is vital that ...(should)...

（约 90 词）

示范填法：How can parents help their children to be well prepared? → 隐藏的问题就是 children are not well prepared（for their education/future）→ 原因 their parents love them too much, or love them in the wrong way → 对策 it is important that parents love their children in the correct way。

### 问法7：说明某事的重要性

（How important... / What is the importance of...）

思维套路：我能简单说"非常重要"吗？这真是我脑子里蹦出的第一个词 → 当然有人会说没那么重要, 他们觉得……确实有道理 → 但我还是觉得…… → 首先对每个**个体**不可或缺, 拿我来说…… → 另外对**社会**也有益, 想象一下如果……就…… → 简言之, 从个人和社会两个角度这事都很重要。

> Can I just say very important? Yeah, because that's the first word popping into my head.（pop into one's head 蹦进脑海）For me, it's kind of a given that ......（a given 顾名思义、明摆着的事, 口语好词）
> Of course, some people might disagree; they might think it's not necessary to ......, and they might think it's because ......
> This may sound reasonable, but I still think it's vital to ......
> First of all, I guess it's indispensable for every individual.（不可或缺;
> 觉得难就换 important）Take me as an example, I think ......
> Also, I think there will also be some societal benefits.（社会效益）Just imagine, if ......, then ......
> So, to sum up, from the two perspectives, both individual and societal, I think it is important that ......

（约 100 词）

## 完整示范

### 完整示范·罗列类：Who do people spend the most time with in their everyday lives?

> Oh yeah, it is true that we spend time with different people every day.（临场改写）I guess this issue is kind of complicated; if I have to create different categories, I mean, for the different people we spend time with, it might be kind of challenging. But based on...（模板）
> **Firstly**, I guess family members. Chinese people believe family reunion is very important, even **paramount**（至关重要）, so we spend as much time as possible with them. I live with my family, so I stay with them for at least 12 hours every day.
> **Secondly**, colleagues, or classmates. For example, my father has worked in the same company for almost 20 years — that's longer than my primary school, middle school and high school combined.
> I know the reality is actually far more complicated, but as far as I know, these two types may be two good examples: family members and colleagues.

共 181 词 ≈ 2 分钟, 其中模板语言 90 词。

### 完整示范·重要性类：How important is it to spend time with different people?

> Can I just say very important? ... For me, it's kind of a given that we should spend time with different people.
> Of course, some people might disagree... they might think it's because **life is limited and we should focus on the ones we like and stick together with them**.（stick together 黏在一起）
> This may sound reasonable, but I still think it's vital to spend quality time with different people.
> First of all... Take me as an example: I socialize with different people not only because I like them, but also because I can learn from them — different people have totally different life stories, and those stories are precious life lessons.
> Also... some societal benefits. Just imagine, if we can all get along better with different people, the society will be more **harmonious**, with fewer arguments and fights, and less **discord**（不和, 高级词）.
> So, to sum up, from the two perspectives...

共 204 词 ≈ 2 分钟, 模板 100 词。老师自评 8 分水平; 用不出高级词、结合部分少一点, 扣到 7 或 6.5 也完全可接受。

## 使用心法

| 心法                 | 说明                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 背模板不会被扣分     | 模板痕迹明显没关系——**扣分只因说错**, 不因"被发现在背"                                             |
| 模板是保底不是天花板 | 考场上对某题**真有自己的看法就先说自己的**; 模板是"原地变哑巴"时的救命稻草——起码保证一半时间在说话 |
| 背熟才有缓冲价值     | 嘴上跑模板, 大脑同步想省略号处填什么——这才是模板真正节约的东西                                     |
| 考场注意力分配       | 模板部分零负担, **全部注意力留给那 50% 的临场发挥**                                                |
