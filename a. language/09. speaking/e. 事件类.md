# Part2 事件类

> 全部文章为讲义逐字原文; **加粗部分 = 母版语言**（来自人物/地点/物件三个母版）, 正常字体 = 结合题目新写的部分

## 核心思想：事件类不另起炉灶

事件类在 Part2 中**占比最高、又多又难**, 但**不需要写新母版**。事件的要素 = 时间、地点、人物 + 起因、经过、高潮、结尾, 其中地点和人物正是已备好的母版——直接从**人物（聪明学长）/ 地点（乡下）/ 物件（蛋糕）**三个母版里挑一篇（或几篇）改造。

> 为什么不学"哈利波特一个故事串50题"？①每道题的改动幅度学生拿不准; ②用的人多必撞车。市面上优秀老师普遍要用 10+ 个故事串 50 题, 这里只用 3 个。

**事件类写作特别注意 ⭐**：要交代**事情发展的脉络**, 用时间提示词把起因-经过-高潮-结尾串出来：at first... / along the way... / in the middle of... / then... / at the climax of... / at last / finally...

### 一次享受的远足

Describe a long walk you enjoyed.

Actually, I hate long walks: they're tiring, they're dull, and you've got nothing to do but keep walking. But, I enjoyed the long walk last year to the top of Taishan Mountain. It's like the most famous mountain in China, very prestigious. Generally, I would never go there myself. But, that time, I had a company, we had the long walk together, and I had a super wonderful time.

**He's the influencer CongMingXueZhang, which can be loosely translated as Mr. Smart. He is an influencer on DouYin, the Chinese version of TikTok. He creates videos about his studying methods and experience.** The whole mountain climbing thing was his idea; he invited his fans to climb the mountain with him, and I was one of the fans.

Actually, I didn't pay much attention to the scenery along the way, cuz I was listening to him all the time. **He shared his studying experiences, studying methods, and even life lessons, sometimes. You can totally tell that he is so smart, it's like everything in studying is a piece of cake to him, he is almost, how to put it, omniscient, I guess.**

I still remember at lunchtime, everybody was kind of tired, and he told us **how he managed to remember 1000 words in three days. I mean, come on, that's more than difficult, it's very difficult, it's a daunting task. But, he did it!** All of the fans **were surprised to hear this, no, we were more than surprised, we were stunned.**

Finally, we reached the top of the mountain. The view was really beautiful, how to say, it was gorgeous, just as the views he revealed for us in the academic field. I am so glad I had the long walk with him, and I still benefit from this experience.
