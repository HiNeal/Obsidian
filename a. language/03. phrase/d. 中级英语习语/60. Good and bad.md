# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| second best | 次优选择 | you shouldn't settle for **second best** | 0 |
| for the best | 结果会更好 | the break-up of their relationship is **for the best** | 0 |
| in a bad way | 状况糟糕 | The company has been **in a bad way** ever since it lost a major order | 0 |
| go one better | 更胜一筹 | Tamsin wanted to **go one better** | 0 |
| think better of ... | 重新考虑后放弃 ... | she **thought better of** a life on the stage | 0 |
| go from bad to worse | 每况愈下 | it has **gone from bad to worse** since then | 0 |
| on ... best behaviour | 表现得规规矩矩 | he has promised to **be on his best behaviour** at the reception tonight | 0 |
| stand ... in good stead | 对 ... 大有裨益 | this would always **stand her in good stead** | 0 |
| the best of both worlds | 两全其美 | gives them **the best of both worlds** | 0 |
| give ... up as a bad job | 认为不值得而放弃 | I've **given it up as a bad job**. | 0 |
| make the best of a bad job | 在逆境中尽力而为 | she **made the best of a bad job** | 0 |
| against ... better judgement | 违背自己的理智判断 | **Against our better judgement**, we agreed that she could go to drama school. | 0 |
| if the worst comes to the worst | 如果最坏的情况发生 | **If the worst comes to the worst**, we'll sell the house | 0 |
