# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| what beats me | 我想不通的是 | **What beats me** is why people are prepared to sit in a traffic jam every morning | 0 |
| get the message | 领会意思 | I think he/she's **got the message**. | 0 |
| food for thought | 值得深思的东西 | the events / your suggestions have certainly **given me food for thought** | 0 |
| learn ... lesson | 吸取教训 | I've **learnt my lesson**. | 0 |
| lose sight of ... | 忽视 ... | The Prime Minister seems to have **lost sight of** why she was elected. | 0 |
| teach ... a lesson | 给 ... 一个教训 | that will **teach him/her a lesson**! | 0 |
| know where ... stand | 清楚自己的处境 | I just want to **know where I stand**, that's all. | 0 |
| set the record straight | 澄清事实 | I just want to **set/put the record straight**. | 0 |
| the ins and outs of ... | ... 的来龙去脉 | I don't know all **the ins and outs of** the situation | 0 |
| hear it on the grapevine | 听到小道消息 | I **heard it on/through the grapevine** that you're thinking of leaving the company. | 0 |
| put it down to experience | 当作一次教训 | I've decided to **put it down to experience**. | 0 |
| a figment of ... imagination | 凭空想象出来的东西 | It's just **a figment of your imagination**. | 0 |
| have second thoughts about ... | 对 ... 改变主意 | I'm **having second thoughts about** moving house. | 0 |
