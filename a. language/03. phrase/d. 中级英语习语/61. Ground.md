# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gain ground | 越来越流行 | The idea that organic food is better ... is **gaining ground**. | 0 |
| give ground | 让步 | neither side would **give ground** | 0 |
| go to ground | 销声匿迹 | she has **gone to ground** | 0 |
| common ground | 共同点 | We share a lot of **common ground** | 0 |
| hold ... ground | 坚守立场 | She was determined to **hold/stand her ground** | 0 |
| stamping ground | 常去的地方 | This part of town was my old **stamping/stomping ground** | 0 |
| shift ... ground | 改变立场 | she keeps **shifting her ground** | 0 |
| prepare the ground | 铺平道路 | **prepares the ground** (for some activity) | 0 |
| on dangerous ground | 涉足危险领域 | I think you're **on dangerous ground** if you try to insist | 0 |
| thick on the ground | 比比皆是 | Good restaurants **are thick/thin on the ground** in this town. | 0 |
| get ... off the ground | 使 ... 起步 | **gets** a project/idea **off the ground** | 0 |
| get in on the ground floor | 一开始就参与 | **gets in on the ground floor** | 0 |
| suit ... down to the ground | 完全适合 ... | Living near the airport **suits me down to the ground** | 0 |
| cut the ground from under ...'s feet | 使 ... 失去立足点 | The Prime Minister's speech has **cut the ground from under the feet of** the Opposition. | 0 |
| wish the ground would swallow ... up | 恨不得找个地缝钻进去 | I just **wished the ground would swallow me up**. | 0 |
