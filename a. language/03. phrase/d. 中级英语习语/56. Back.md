# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| be on ...'s back | 对 ... 唠叨不休 | My parents are **always on my back** about doing my homework on time. | 0 |
| take a back seat | 退居次要位置 | If you **take a back seat** | 0 |
| by the back door | 通过非正当途径 | something comes **by/through the back door** | 0 |
| put ...'s back up | 惹恼 ... | I **put her back up** when I criticised Americans | 0 |
| on the back burner | 暂时搁置 | If a plan **is on the back burner** | 0 |
| bend over backwards | 竭尽全力 | I had **bent over backwards** to please her | 0 |
| stab ... in the back | 背后捅刀 | I suspect she'd happily **stab me in the back**. | 0 |
| at the back of beyond | 偏僻之地 | If somewhere is **at/in the back of beyond** | 0 |
| when ...'s back is turned | 趁 ... 不注意 | As soon as the teacher's **back was turned**, the children started passing notes | 0 |
| without a backward glance | 头也不回地 | she **left without a backward glance** | 0 |
| fall off the back of a lorry | 来路不明 | something **came/fell off the back of a lorry** | 0 |
| be glad to see the back of ... | 很高兴看到 ... 离开 | I'll be **glad to see the back of** this government. | 0 |
| with one hand tied behind ... back | 轻而易举 | I **could've done it with one hand tied behind my back**! | 0 |
| know ... like the back of ... hand | 对 ... 了如指掌 | he **knows the town like the back of his hand** | 0 |
| you scratch my back and I'll scratch yours | 互相帮忙 | **You scratch my back and I'll scratch yours.** | 0 |
