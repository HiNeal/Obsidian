# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| if need be | 必要的话 | **If need be**, I'll work in a fast food place | 0 |
| duty bound | 有责任 | I feel **duty bound** to tell you that all is not well | 0 |
| take ... pick | 随便挑 | **Take your pick.** There are three different ones over there | 0 |
| you're in luck | 你走运了 | **You're in luck.** We have just one copy left. | 0 |
| be dying to ... | 迫不及待要 ... | I'm really really looking forward to meeting your cousin. **I'm dying to** meet her. | 0 |
| do ... own thing | 做自己想做的事 | I just wanted to **do my own thing**. | 0 |
| wouldn't go amiss | 会很有用 | an extra computer **wouldn't go amiss** | 0 |
| in keeping with ... | 与 ... 相称 | They were not **in keeping with** the tone of things. | 0 |
| not worth ... while | 不值得 | It's **not worth my while** buying a new car this month. | 0 |
| give anything to ... | 愿意付出一切去 ... | **I'd give anything** to shake his hand. | 0 |
| wouldn't be seen dead | 打死也不愿意 | I **wouldn't be seen dead** riding one. | 0 |
| if push comes to shove | 万不得已时 | **if push comes to shove**, I'll just have to leave | 0 |
| need ... like a hole in the head | 根本不需要 | I **need** a trip to London **like I need a hole in the head**. | 0 |
| wouldn't touch ... with a barge pole | 绝对不碰 | I **wouldn't touch it with a barge pole**. | 0 |
