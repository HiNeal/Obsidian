# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| at home | 自在 | If you **are/feel at home** somewhere, you are/feel comfortable there. | 0 |
| hit the ceiling | 暴跳如雷 | Alf **hit the ceiling/roof** this morning for no reason at all. | 0 |
| come home to ... | 使 ... 深刻领悟 | The implications of what she had said didn't **come home to me** until some days later. | 0 |
| sit on the fence | 保持中立观望 | If you **sit on the fence**, you delay making a decision | 0 |
| as safe as houses | 绝对安全 | if something is **as safe as houses**, it is extremely safe | 0 |
| bring home to ... | 使 ... 明白 | Her news reports have really **brought home to me** the horrors of war. | 0 |
| fly off the handle | 大发雷霆 | Her father **flew off the handle** when she said she wasn't going to return to university. | 0 |
| hold the key to ... | 是 ... 的关键 | Female voters **hold the key to** the party's success in the election. | 0 |
| make ...self at home | 不拘束 | If you **make yourself at home**, you relax and make yourself comfortable. | 0 |
| put ... in the picture | 向 ... 说明情况 | I'm glad she **put me in the picture** | 0 |
| get ... foot in the door | 获得入门机会 | at least I've **got my foot in the door** | 0 |
| burn the candle at both ends | 过度劳累 | he's been **burning the candle at both ends** because of his exams | 0 |
| take a leaf out of ...'s book | 效仿 ... | I wish he'd **take a leaf out of his girlfriend's book** | 0 |
| get out of bed on the wrong side | 一大早就心情不好 | I thought he'd just **got out of bed on the wrong side** | 0 |
| come down on one side or the other | 选边站 | you have to **come down on one side or the other** | 0 |
