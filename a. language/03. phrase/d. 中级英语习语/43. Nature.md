# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| in the air | 在流传 | There was a sense of relief **in the air** when the 'not guilty' verdict was announced. | 0 |
| upper-crust | 上流社会的 | a person is **upper-crust** | 0 |
| in the dark | 蒙在鼓里 | If you are **in the dark** or someone **keeps you in the dark** | 0 |
| up in the air | 悬而未决 | Whether the government will change the law on football hooliganism is **up in the air** | 0 |
| clear the air | 消除隔阂 | they have helped to **clear the air** to a certain extent | 0 |
| down-to-earth | 务实的 | someone is a **down-to-earth** person | 0 |
| bite the dust | 失败 | an idea or plan/project **bites the dust** | 0 |
| over the moon | 欣喜若狂 | you are **over the moon** about something | 0 |
| in ... element | 如鱼得水 | If you are **in your element** | 0 |
| out of the blue | 出乎意料 | the news had come **out of the blue** | 0 |
| hit rock bottom | 跌到谷底 | something **is/hits rock bottom** | 0 |
| on another planet | 不切实际 | someone is **(living) on another planet** | 0 |
| out of ... element | 不得其所 | If you are **out of your element** | 0 |
| a breath of fresh air | 令人耳目一新 | The news has come as **a breath of fresh air** for students | 0 |
| find out how the land lies | 摸清形势 | you **find out how the land lies** | 0 |
| come down to earth with a bang | 猛然回到现实 | you **come (back) down to earth with a bang** | 0 |
