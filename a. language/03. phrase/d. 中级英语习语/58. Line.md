# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| out of line | 越界 | he said something **out of line** at a meeting | 0 |
| line of work | 职业 | at least not in his **line of work** | 0 |
| draw the line | 划清界限 | You have to **draw the line** somewhere. | 0 |
| in line for ... | 有望得到 ... | Imran knew he was **in line for** promotion last year. | 0 |
| drop ... a line | 给 ... 写封短信 | **Drop me a line** when you have a spare moment. | 0 |
| the bottom line | 关键所在 | **The bottom line** is that children must be protected. | 0 |
| step out of line | 越轨 | it is not a good idea to **step out of line** | 0 |
| on the right lines | 方向正确 | he seems to **be going along/on the right lines** now | 0 |
| along the same lines | 类似地 | saying anything **along/on the same lines** again | 0 |
| draw the line at ... | 绝不做 ... | I **draw the line at** body-piercing! | 0 |
| draw a line under ... | 对 ... 到此为止 | Let's **draw a line under** this episode | 0 |
| along the lines of ... | 大致像 ... | it was something **along the lines of** the problems of the company | 0 |
| read between the lines | 揣摩言外之意 | **Reading between the lines**, I think he's feeling a little lonely. | 0 |
| sign on the dotted line | 签字同意 | It is foolish to **sign on the dotted line** until you have checked all the details. | 0 |
| a fine line between ... and ... | ... 与 ... 仅一线之隔 | There is **a fine line between** determination and pig-headedness. | 0 |
