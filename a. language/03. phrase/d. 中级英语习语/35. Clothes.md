# 短语

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| pocket ... | 私吞(钱款) | He sold them for £60 and **pocketed** £10! | 0 |
| out of pocket | 倒贴 | I actually ended up £35 **out of pocket**. | 0 |
| wear the trousers | 当家作主 | my mum **wears the trousers** in our family! | 0 |
| keep ... shirt on | 别生气 | **Keep your shirt on!** | 0 |
| have it in the bag | 稳操胜券 | I'm sure I **have it in the bag**. | 0 |
| roll up ... sleeves | 卷起袖子大干 | So **roll up your sleeves** and let's get busy! | 0 |
| wear different hats | 身兼数职 | Our teacher **wears lots of different hats**. | 0 |
| at the drop of a hat | 毫不迟疑地 | She will come **at the drop of a hat** and help you. | 0 |
| have big shoes to fill | 难以胜任前任的角色 | The new manager will **have big shoes to fill**. | 0 |
| bore the pants off ... | 让 ... 无聊透顶 | The professor **bored the pants off** everyone in the hall. | 0 |
| take ... hat off to ... | 向 ... 表示敬意 | I **take my hat off to** her. | 0 |
