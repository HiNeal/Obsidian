# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| grab a snack | 随便吃点东西 | We can **grab a snack** before the meeting | 0 |
| look into ... | 调查 ... | The police are investigating / **looking into** the arms deal. | 0 |
| haven't a clue | 毫无头绪 | I **haven't a clue** what you mean. | 0 |
| totally awesome | 超级棒的(非正式) | That film was **totally awesome**! | 0 |
| the foggiest idea | 丝毫的概念 | I haven't **the foggiest idea** what you mean. | 0 |
| request an extension | 申请延期(正式) | Students may **request an extension** after consulting their tutor. | 0 |
| ask for an extension | 请求延期 | You can **ask for an extension** after you've talked to your tutor | 0 |
| have a word with ... | 和 ... 谈一谈 | you've **had a word with** your tutor | 0 |
| dig out info about ... | 挖出关于 ... 的信息(非正式) | The cops are trying to **dig out info about** the arms deal. | 0 |
| submit ... assignments | 提交作业 | Students must **submit** their **assignments** by 1 May. | 0 |
| hand in ... assignments | 交作业 | You have to **hand in** your **assignments** by 1 May. | 0 |
| subject to police investigation | 受警方调查(法律用语) | The arms deal may be **subject to police investigation**. | 0 |
| conduct an investigation into ... | 对 ... 展开调查(正式) | The police are **conducting an investigation into** the arms deal. | 0 |
