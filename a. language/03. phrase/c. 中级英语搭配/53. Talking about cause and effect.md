# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cause chaos | 造成混乱 | The storm **caused chaos** / havoc / a lot of damage. | 0 |
| cause alarm | 引起恐慌 | Her remarks **caused alarm**/concern. | 0 |
| create problems | 制造问题 | Your presence on the committee is **creating problems** for all of us. | 0 |
| spark off riots | 引发骚乱 | The announcement **sparked off riots/demonstrations** in the cities. | 0 |
| attract support | 获得支持 | The charity appeal **attracted support** from a wide range of people. | 0 |
| produce results | 产生结果 | Her research has not yet **produced any results** | 0 |
| adverse effects | 不良影响 | The **negative/adverse effects** of the changes were not noticed immediately. | 0 |
| cause an uproar | 引起轩然大波 | The book **caused an uproar** in the United States. | 0 |
| effect a change | 实现改变 | We are trying to **effect a change** in the way people think about their diet. | 0 |
| spark off rumours | 引发谣言 | His wife's absence **sparked off rumours** in the media. | 0 |
| attract criticism | 招致批评 | His book has **attracted** a lot of **criticism/interest**. | 0 |
| reduce the impact | 减少影响 | The government hopes to **reduce/minimise the impact** of the new taxes. | 0 |
| cause a sensation | 引起轰动 | Their love affair **caused a sensation**. | 0 |
| affect ... deeply | 深深影响 ... | Her death **affected** everyone **deeply**. | 0 |
| unexpected outcome | 意外结果 | The new measures had an **unexpected/unforeseen outcome**. | 0 |
| inevitable outcome | 必然结果 | the **predictable/inevitable outcome** that many people became poorer | 0 |
| beneficial effects | 有益的影响 | The **positive/beneficial effects** of the changes were soon apparent. | 0 |
| cause great anxiety | 引起极大焦虑 | Her son's behaviour **caused her great anxiety** | 0 |
| the immediate cause | 直接原因 | The **immediate cause** of the problem was an oil leak. | 0 |
| the underlying cause | 根本原因 | The **underlying cause** of the problem was a lack of funds | 0 |
| an unforeseen result | 意外后果 | One **indirect/unforeseen result** of the new laws has been a rise in unemployment. | 0 |
| strengthen the impact | 增强影响 | **strengthened/increased the impact** of her report | 0 |
| create awareness of ... | 提高对 ... 的认识 | Her book helped **create awareness** of inner-city poverty. | 0 |

| the direct result of ... | ... 的直接结果 | The crisis was the **direct/inevitable result** of bad economic planning. | 0 |

| have a major impact on ... | 对 ... 产生重大影响 | Latino singers have **had a major impact** on pop music this year. | 0 |
| produce the opposite effect | 产生相反的效果 | My comments **produced the opposite effect** to what I intended. | 0 |
| have a lot of influence on ... | 对 ... 影响很大 | The drug companies **have a lot of influence** on doctors. | 0 |
| bring about the downfall of ... | 导致 ... 垮台 | The events **brought about the downfall/collapse** of the government. | 0 |
