# 短语

| 短语 | 释义 | 意群 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| do sport | 做运动 | You **do** or **play sport**. (NOT make sport) | 0 |
| go skiing | 去滑雪 | You **go skiing**. (NOT make skiing) | 0 |
| go to judo | 去上柔道课 | You can also say you **go to aerobics/judo/yoga/karate** | 0 |
| train hard | 刻苦训练 | athletes **train hard** | 0 |
| home match | 主场比赛 | It might be a **home** or an **away match** | 0 |
| win a match | 赢得比赛 | Sportsmen and sportswomen want to **win matches** | 0 |
| home ground | 主场 | playing on your team's **home ground** | 0 |
| lose a match | 输掉比赛 | not **lose matches** | 0 |
| gain a place | 取得名额 | to **gain/get a place** in the next stage | 0 |
| have a match | 进行一场比赛 | You can **play** or **have a (football) match** | 0 |
| score a goal | 进球 | The aim is to **score a goal**. | 0 |
| take the lead | 取得领先 | they will have **taken the lead** by half-time | 0 |
| do activities | 开展活动 | You **do activities**. | 0 |
| deserve to win | 应该获胜 | Sometimes a team or player **deserves to win** | 0 |
| put up a fight | 奋力抗争 | They are likely to **put up a fight** | 0 |
| take a penalty | 罚点球 | an opponent **taking a penalty** | 0 |
| drop the player | 让球员下场 | the manager may decide to **drop the player** from the team | 0 |
| training session | 训练课 | They probably attend/do at least five **training sessions** a week. | 0 |
| blow ... whistle | 吹哨 | the referee **blowing his whistle** | 0 |
| take a free kick | 罚任意球 | or **taking a free kick** | 0 |
| narrowly defeated | 惜败 | but gets **narrowly defeated/beaten** | 0 |
| fail a drugs test | 药检不合格 | when they **fail a drugs test** | 0 |
| foul ... opponent | 对对手犯规 | you **foul your opponent** | 0 |
| fierce competition | 激烈的竞争 | come up against **fierce/intense competition** | 0 |
| tackle an opponent | 抢断对手 | Players may **tackle an opponent** | 0 |
| enter a competition | 参加比赛 | Before they **go in for / enter a competition** | 0 |
| world record holder | 世界纪录保持者 | become a **world record holder** | 0 |
| come up against ... | 遭遇 ... | They are sure to **come up against** fierce/intense competition | 0 |
| play computer games | 玩电脑游戏 | You **play computer** or other **games**. | 0 |
| play a game of cards | 玩牌 | You **have/play a game of cards**. | 0 |
| achieve ... ambitions | 实现抱负 | as they try to **achieve their ambitions** | 0 |
| bring on a substitute | 换上替补 | or **bring on a substitute** | 0 |
| break the world record | 打破世界纪录 | a sportsperson's ultimate aim is to **break the world record** | 0 |
| set a new world record | 创造新的世界纪录 | they **set a new world record** | 0 |
| achieve a personal best | 取得个人最好成绩 | if they just **achieve a personal best** | 0 |
| enhance ... performance | 提高成绩 | to **enhance their performance** | 0 |
| practise taking a penalty | 练习罚点球 | you might **practise taking a penalty** | 0 |
| take possession of the ball | 控球 | to **take possession of the ball** | 0 |
| the next round of the competition | 比赛的下一轮 | the next **stage/round of the competition** | 0 |
