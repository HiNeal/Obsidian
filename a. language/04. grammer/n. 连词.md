# 连词分类

| **连词分类** | **意思** | **例子** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 并列连词 | 连接语法地位平等的词、词组或句子 | The rabbit is smart and cute. |
| 从属连词 | 连接主句和从句 | When I arrived, the rabbit was eating. |

# 并列连词

## 并列

| **并列**               | **短语**                                                                                               | **释义**                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| and                    | The rabbit is smart **and** cute.                                                                      | 和; 并且                                           |
| both...and...          | **Both** the rabbit **and** the wolf like carrots.                                                     | 两者都……                                           |
| not only...but also... | The rabbit **not only** teaches English **but also** makes videos.                                     | 不仅……而且……                                       |
| not just...but also... | These barriers **not just** make crossings longer **but also** divide communities.                     | 不仅……还……                                         |
| neither...nor...       | The rabbit likes **neither** apples **nor** pears.                                                     | 既不……也不……                                       |
| nor                    | The rabbit doesn't like apples, **nor** does he like pears.<br><br>**Nor is it only** about the young. | 也不……（否定并列连词, 置于分句句首时引起部分倒装） |

## 选择

| **选择**       | **短语**                                        | **释义**     |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| or             | Apples **or** carrots?                          | 或者         |
| either...or... | **Either** the rabbit **or** the wolf is going. | 要么……要么…… |
| or else        | Hurry up, **or else** you'll be late.           | 否则; 要不然 |

## 转折

| **转折**               | **短语**                                              | **释义**                                 |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| but                    | The rabbit cooked carrots, **but** he didn't eat any. | 但是                                     |
| yet                    | The rabbit cooked carrots, **yet** he didn't eat any. | 然而; 但是                               |
| not ... but ...        | The aim is **not** speed **but** accuracy.            | 不是……而是……                             |
| not ... but rather ... | The aim is **not** speed **but rather** accuracy.     | 不是……而是……（用于进一步纠正或强调后者） |

⚠️ yet 的双重身份：I haven't eaten yet, yet I'm not hungry.（第一个 yet 是副词"还", 第二个 yet 是连词"但"）

## 因果

| **因果** | **短语**                                         | **释义**             |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| so       | The rabbit was busy, **so** he won't go.         | 所以（前因后果）     |
| for      | The rabbit must be hungry, **for** he ate a lot. | 因为（前果后因推测） |

⚠️ 就近原则：neither...nor、either...or、not only...but also 连接主语时, 动词与最近的主语一致

> Neither the rabbit nor the wolf likes apples.（wolf 是单数 → likes）
> Either the rabbit or I am going to the party.（I → am）
> Not only the rabbit but also his friends are going.（friends → are）

# 从属连词

| **引导名词从句** | **短语**                                                                     | **释义**         |

| that             | **That** the rabbit ate the carrot was obvious.                              | 无实义           |

# 同词多义


**while** 

- **时间关系（当……时; 在……期间）**：两件事在时间轴上延续、同时发生（= as / during the time that）

>While the rabbit was reading, I took a nap.

- **对比关系（而; 然而）**：客观平行展示两个事物的差异（= whereas）

>In some versions, the wolf swallows up the grandmother, **while** in others it locks her in a cupboard.
   >
>In some stories Red Riding Hood gets the better of the wolf on her own, **while** in others a hunter or a woodcutter hears her cries and comes to her rescue.

- **让步关系（虽然; 尽管）**：承认事实但主旨重心在后半句（= although / even though）

> **While** I admit his cleverness, I don't trust him.
