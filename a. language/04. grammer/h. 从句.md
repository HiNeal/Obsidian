# 为什么英语从句这么难

**核心原因：中英思维差异。**

中文定语一律前置——形容词/句子 + "的", 全部堆在被修饰词前面：
> 好吃的胡萝卜 → 我买来的胡萝卜 → 我昨天在菜市场刚从地里回来的乌龟那买来的胡萝卜

英文：单词定语前置（a tasty carrot）, 句子定语后置（a carrot **that I bought**）。

**后果**：中国学生翻译时, "兔子在吃一根我买来的胡萝卜" → "The rabbit is eating I bought a carrot" → 卡住了。

中文定语太长会头重脚轻（说了半天才知是兔子）, 英文后置不怕长, 但多了"关系词"概念, 中国人不习惯——这就是难点根源。


# 从句原理

一个句子 A 成为另一个句子的某种句子成分：

```
我看见 [兔子吃了根胡萝卜]。
       └── 大宾语 ──┘
I saw [that the rabbit ate a carrot].
```

- **A = 从句（subordinate clause）**：兔子吃了胡萝卜 → 从属、服从、不能独立成句
- **B = 主句（main clause）**：我看见……
- A + B = **复杂句（complex sentence）**


# 句子类型

| 类型 | 英文 | 结构 | 例子 |
| ----------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **简单句**              | Simple Sentence   | 不能再拆                         | The rabbit ate a carrot.                            |
| **复合句 / 并列句**     | Compound Sentence | 简单句 + 连词 + 简单句, 不分主次 | The rabbit was hungry, **so** he ate a carrot.      |
| **复杂句 / 主从复合句** | Complex Sentence  | 主句 + 从句, 分主次              | **Because** he was hungry, the rabbit ate a carrot. |

> ⚠️ **术语分歧**：国内很多教材把 Compound Sentence 译为"并列句", Complex Sentence 译为"复合句"。英语兔建议用"**主从复合句**"和"**并列复合句**"区分, 避免混淆。


# 从句分类

| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| <br><br>**名词从句**         | <br><br>名词性从句        | <br><br>大名词         | 主语从句<br>宾语从句<br>表语从句<br>同位语从句<br>宾补从句              |
| **形容词从句**                | 定语从句/关系从句            | 大形容词                | 定语从句                                               |

# 定语从句（形容词从句 / 关系从句）

英语从句中最重要、内容最多、可以说最难的一类。

## 中英思维差异（核心）

**中文思维**：定语前置
> 兔子在吃一根 [我买来的] 胡萝卜。
> 好吃的胡萝卜 / 兔子吃了的胡萝卜 / 一个是兔子的老师 / 一个我昨天看见的老师 / 兔子吃胡萝卜的地方 / 兔子吃胡萝卜的原因

**英文思维**：先行词 + 关系词 + 从句（后置）
> a tasty carrot
> a carrot **that the rabbit ate**
> a teacher **who is a rabbit**
> a teacher **whom I saw yesterday**
> The place **where** the rabbit ate the carrot
> The reason **why** the rabbit ate the carrot

## 结构

```
先行词 + 关系词 + 从句（充当定语）
 carrot   that     I bought
```

## 构造方法

把陈述句中被修饰的词换成关系代词, 移到从句开头：
1. I bought the carrot. → I bought **that**
2. 移到开头 → **that** I bought
3. 放在先行词后 → a carrot **that I bought**

## 关系代词

| 关系代词 | 先行词 | 句中角色 | 构造来源 | 例子 |
| --------- | ------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **who**   | 人     | 主语      | The teacher ate a carrot → **Who** ate a carrot          | The teacher **who** ate a carrot is a rabbit.              |
| **whom**  | 人     | 宾语      | I saw the teacher → I saw **whom**                       | The teacher **whom** I saw is a rabbit.                    |
| **whose** | 人/物  | 所有格    | The teacher's favorite food → **Whose** favorite food    | The teacher **whose** favorite food is carrot is a rabbit. |
| **which** | 物     | 主语/宾语 | The teacher likes the food → The teacher likes **which** | The food **which** the teacher likes is a carrot.          |
| **that**  | 人/物  | 主语/宾语 | —                                                        | The carrot **that** I bought is tasty.                     |

## that vs which

**只能用 that 的情况**（先行词唯一确定、没有选择余地）：

> The rabbit ate the **largest** carrot **that** I've ever seen.（最高级）
> **All** the rabbits **that** ate a carrot.（不定代词）
> The **only** rabbit **that** ate a carrot.（唯一）
> The **first** rabbit **that** ate a carrot.（序数词）

**that 和 which 都可以的情况**（同类事物中按条件限定选择）：
> The rabbit ate a carrot **which/that** I bought.
>（胡萝卜千千万, 这兔子吃的是哪一根？哦, 我买的那一根。）

**记法**：连提问都不用就知道是谁 → 只能 that。如果还能问 "Which one?" → that/which 都行。

## 关系副词

| 关系副词 | 例子 | 相当于 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| **when**    | This was the day **when** the rabbit ate the carrot for the first time.  | on which    |
| **where**   | This was the place **where** the rabbit ate the carrot.                  | at/in which |
| **why**     | This is the reason **why** the rabbit ate the carrot.                    | for which   |
| **whereby** | working practices **whereby** workers learn through the algorithm's eyes | by which    |

> **关系副词 = 介词 + 关系代词 which**, 因此两者可以互换：
> - the place **where** he ate = the place **at which** he ate
> - the reason **why** he left = the reason **for which** he left
>
> 当先行词没有对应的关系副词时（如 extent、way）, 只能直接用「介词 + which」：
> - the extent **to which**（technology influences...）→ 还原自：influences... **to the extent**
> - the way **in which**（he spoke）→ 还原自：he spoke **in the way**
>
> ⚠️ 正式书面（雅思/学术）优先用介词前置：`to which` / `in which`; 口语可介词后置：`which...to`

## 限定性 vs 非限定性

**限定性**：限定范围, 去掉句意改变
> The rabbit ate the carrot **which was on the table**.
>（好多胡萝卜, 限定：在桌上的那根）

> I have a head **which is round**.
>（限定性：我有好几个头, 其中一个是圆的。其他可能是方的扁的）

**非限定性**：补充信息, 去掉句意不变。加逗号, 不用 that。
> I have a head, which is round.
>（非限定性：我有一个头, 它是圆的 → 接着说话而已, 换了个代词避免重复）

非限定性 = 以前以后表达两个信息, 用代词接着说话。

> My head, **which is big**, is useful when it rains.（插入语, 去掉不影响句意）

**套娃结构**（语法可行但不推荐）：
> This is a rabbit **who started uploading videos since a few months ago to focus on English teaching and is liked by many students**.

⚠️ 中英文都不推荐无限套娃, 更好的方式是拆句。

> The biggest carrot, which the rabbit ate, was on the table.（最大的 + 非限定 → 不冲突, 因为 which 只是补充）

⚠️ 但不能说：The biggest carrot **which** the rabbit ate...（会被误解为限定性, 与 biggest 的唯一性冲突）

**非限定性可指代整个主句（as vs which）**：

非限定性定语从句中的关系代词 **which** 和 **as** 都可以指代前面整个主句的内容, 但在逻辑语气和语序位置上有明确区别：

| 关系词 | 核心逻辑语气 | 位置灵活性 | 经典例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **as** | **“正如……那样”**<br>（表举例佐证、先例印证、符合预期、众所周知） | 可置于 **句首、句中或句末** | - *The rabbit ate the carrot, **as was expected**.*（正如预期的那样）<br><br>- *The public can sue businesses, **as happened** after the disaster.*（正如灾难后发生的那样——举例佐证）<br><br>- ***As is known to all**, rabbits love carrots.*（众所周知）<br><br>- ***As Yong explains in his book**, we mess with them at our peril.*（正如勇在书中所阐述的那样） |
| **which** | **“这/那件事……”**<br>（客观陈述事实、结果推导或补充评价） | **只能置于主句之后** | - *The rabbit ate the carrot, **which was not surprising**.*（which = 前面整件事"兔子吃了胡萝卜"）<br><br>- *The rabbit missed the train, **which made him upset**.*（兔子错过了火车, 这让他很沮丧） |

> ⚠️ **高频避坑（雅思写作高频错误）**：
> 1. **不可多加代词主语**：`as` 本身就是从句的主语（或宾语）, **绝对不要写成 `as it happened` 或 `as it was expected`**;
> 2. **固定评注性结构**：`as is well known`（众所周知）、`as was mentioned above`（正如前文所提及的）、`as sb explains / points out`（正如某人所阐述/指出）、`as often happens`（正如常发生的那样）、`as follows`（如下）。



# 名词从句

## 主语从句

**原理**：把主语换成一个从句。

**确定信息**：
> **That the rabbit ate a carrot** is obvious.（that 是信号, 英美人士一听就知从句开始）
> It is obvious **that the rabbit ate a carrot**.（形式主语 it）

**为什么要有形式主语**？主语从句太长 → 头重脚轻：
> ❌ That the hungry rabbit had already eaten the carrot for dinner this evening is obvious.
> ✅ It is obvious **that the hungry rabbit had already eaten the carrot for dinner this evening**.

中文翻译："很明显, 兔子今天晚饭已经吃了根胡萝卜" 或 "兔子今天晚饭已经吃了根胡萝卜, 这很明显"。

**不确定信息**（连接代词/副词引导）：

> **Whether** the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious.（是否）
> **Where** the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious.（哪里）
> **When** the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious.（什么时候）
> **How** the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious.（怎样）
> **Who** ate the carrot is obvious.（谁）
> **What** the rabbit ate is obvious.（什么）

对应形式主语版本完全相同结构调整。注意：引导词后语序与陈述句一致, 和中文也基本一致。

**与虚拟语气的关联**：

> It is suggested / demanded / insisted / ordered **that you (should) eat a carrot**.
> It is important / essential / necessary **that you (should) eat a carrot**.

（这些句子都是主语从句, 只不过谓语动词触发虚拟语气）

## 宾语从句

**确定信息**：
> I saw **that the rabbit ate a carrot**.

**不确定信息**：
> I saw **whether / who / what / where / when / how** the rabbit ate the carrot.

⚠️ **重点一：从句语序 = 引导词 + 正常陈述语序, 不要加助动词！**
> ✅ I know **where the rabbit ate the carrot**.
> ❌ I know **where did the rabbit eat the carrot**.

（看到 where/when/how 别以为是问句乱加助动词）

⚠️ **重点二：that 的省略**

口语/非正式中 that 常可省略：
> I know (that) the rabbit ate the carrot.

**不能省略的情况**：
- 多个并列宾语从句的第二个 that：
  > I didn't know that the rabbit ate the carrot **and that** you saw it（第二个 that 不可省, 否则断句为 you saw it）
- 有插入语隔开：
  > I know, just like you do, **that** the rabbit ate the carrot.（不可省）

**否定转移**：think, believe, expect 等词的否定要放在主句动词上：
> ✅ I **don't think** that the rabbit is smart.
> ❌ I think that the rabbit **isn't** smart.

（否定在从句里也是对的, 只是不自然。绝大多数情况下否定前移。）

**时态一致**：主句过去 → 从句也过去（客观真理例外）：
> I **didn't think** that you **were** right.（主句过去→从句也过去）
> I **knew** that the rabbit **liked** carrots.（习惯动作也要用过去）
> I knew that the sun **rises** in the east.（客观真理例外; 用 rose 反而暗示"太阳过去从东边升起, 以后不一定了"）

## B + 形容词 + that 从句（归类争议）

> I am **sure** that there is a carrot on the table.
> I am **happy** that there is a carrot on the table.
> I am **afraid** that there is no carrot on the table.

三种归类观点：

1. **宾语从句**：相当于 I confirm / like / fear that...
2. **同位语从句**：相当于 I am sure **of the fact** that...
3. **状语从句（原因）**：I am happy **because**...

英语兔倾向宾语从句。把上述句子理解为同位语的理由：

> I am sure of the fact **that** there is a carrot on the table.
> I am happy about the fact **that** there is a carrot on the table.
> I am afraid of the fact **that** there is no carrot on the table.

这样看 that 从句实际上相当于同位语。但原句毕竟没有 fact 之类的词, 所以稍显牵强。学术上无定论, 不影响实际使用。

## 表语从句（主语补语从句）

系动词后面, 用从句充当表语。

**最常见的系动词是 be**：
> The problem is **that the rabbit is hungry**.
> The reason is **that there is no more carrot**.
> The question is **whether the rabbit is hungry**.
> The question is **what / where / when / how** the rabbit should eat the carrot.

**其他系动词**：feel, seem, look, taste 等：

> It **seems** that the rabbit is hungry.（it 是形式主语）
> It **feels** that you're not telling the truth.

这些句子中 it 没有实际意义, 只是占个位置, 真正要表达的全部在后面的从句里——后面的从句是表语从句。

表语从句往往就是把主语从句移到系动词之后。

## 同位语从句

抽象名词用从句"再说一遍"。

> The fact **that the rabbit ate the carrot** did not surprise me.
> The question **whether the rabbit would eat the carrot** is on all our minds.
> I have no idea **who ate the carrot**.（idea 是宾语, who 从句补充说明 → 宾语的同位语）

中文必须加"这件事实""这个问题"等标志, 因为中文没有引导词表示从句开始。

## 宾语补语从句

复杂及物动词（call, make, consider 等）的宾补由从句充当。

> You can call me **what you like**.（叫我你想叫的 → 随便叫我什么）
> My education made me **who I am today**.（教育造就了今天的我 → 成就了今天的我）

相当于把形容词宾补（made me smart）换成了从句。

⚠️ 国内语法体系往往略过宾补从句。它不是纯粹的名词性, 也可能是形容词性。


# 副词从句（状语从句）

## 时间状语从句

**before — 在某一时间点之前**
> The rabbit ate a carrot at home **before the wolf stopped by**.

从句可提前（加逗号）：
> **Before the wolf stopped by**, the rabbit ate a carrot at home.

**when / while / as — 在某一时间点之中**

**when**：强调**时间点**, 常有"突然"之意
> The rabbit was eating a carrot **when I reached home**.
> The rabbit was about to eat a carrot **when the wolf stopped by**.（突然来访！）

**while**：强调**一个时间段内**
> The rabbit was eating a carrot **while I was making a video**.

**as**：强调**两个动作同时进行**
> I ate a carrot **as I made the video**.（一边吃一边做）

**after — 在某一时间点之后**
> The rabbit ate a carrot **after the wolf stopped by**.

**since — 从之前某时间点算起（对之后有影响, 常用完成时态, 暗示对现在有影响）**

**时间用法**：
> The rabbit **has shared** three carrots **since the wolf stopped by**.
> The rabbit **has not stopped** sharing carrots **since the wolf stopped by**.

⚠️ since 也可以引导**原因状语从句**（不是时间）, 需要根据上下文区分：
> **Since** you are hungry, you can eat the carrot.（既然你饿了 = 原因）
> ≠ 自从你饿了的时间算起…（误读为时间）

**until — 直到之后某一时间点**
> The rabbit waited **until the wolf stopped by**.
> The rabbit didn't eat any carrot **until the wolf stopped by**.（直到…才）

**as soon as — 一…就…**
> The rabbit will eat a carrot **as soon as the wolf leaves**.

**the next time — 下次…时**
> The rabbit will share the biggest carrot **the next time the wolf stops by**.

## 地点状语从句

**主要引导词**：where

> The rabbit ate the carrot **where he found it**.

⚠️ **where 从句的类型取决于在句中的角色**：
> The rabbit ate the carrot where he found it. → **状语从句**（直接 = 地点）
> The rabbit ate the carrot at the place where he found it. → **定语从句**（修饰 place）
> This is where he found the carrot. → **表语从句**
> Where he found the carrot is a secret. → **主语从句**

**强调形式**：

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例子 |
| -------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **wherever**   | 不管哪里   | The rabbit will go **wherever** he can find a carrot. |
| **everywhere** | 所有地方   | The rabbit sees a carrot **everywhere** he goes.      |
| **anywhere**   | 任何某一处 | The rabbit can eat a carrot **anywhere** he likes.    |

## 比较状语从句

**than — 更…**（正式形式是完整从句, 口语常省略）：
> 正式：He is smarter **than I am**. / This carrot is bigger **than that one is**.
> 口语：He is smarter than me. / This carrot is bigger than that one.

**as…as — 同样…**

正式（完整从句）：
> He is **as** smart **as I am**.
> This carrot is **as** big **as that one is**.
> She is **as** beautiful **as Snow White is**.

口语/省略：
> He is as smart as me.
> This carrot is as big as that one.
> She is as beautiful as Snow White.

**the + 比较级, the + 比较级 — 越…越…**：
> **The more** you practice English, **the more fluent** you become.
> **The more coins** you give me, **the more videos** I make.

## 条件状语从句

**if — 如果**

**真实条件句**：
> **If** the rabbit sees a carrot, he will eat it.

**虚拟条件句**（用动词虚拟语气）：
> **If** I saw a carrot, I **would eat** it.
> **If** I had seen a carrot, I **would have eaten** it.

**「主将从现」原理**

「主将从现」口诀只能覆盖真实条件句, 无法解释虚拟条件句——被英语兔批评为"最模糊的总结之一"。

**真正原理**："如果我**将会**看见胡萝卜" → 矛盾！条件本身不确定是否会形成 → 只能用**一般状态**（去掉时间信息）。

> 中文同理：我们说"如果我**看见**一根胡萝卜"（不是"如果我**将会看见**"）

规律：**条件从句总是比主句在时态上落后一个时间段**：

| 条件从句（从句）           | 主句                      | 时态关系                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| If I **see** a carrot      | I **will eat** it         | 现在 → 将来             |
| If I **saw** a carrot      | I **would eat** it        | 过去 → 过去将来         |
| If I **had seen** a carrot | I **would have eaten** it | 过去完成 → 过去将来完成 |

⚠️ 虚拟语气中的过去式不是时间上的过去, 而是表达"与现实相反"。

**unless — 除非（把 if 反着说）**

> **If** you don't like this video, you will not give it a thumbs up.
> = **Unless** you like this video, you will not give it a thumbs up.

**其他条件引导词**

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例子 |
| ------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| provided that | 如果（正式） | **Provided that** my video is excellent, you will give it a thumbs up. |
| as long as    | 只要         | **As long as** I make excellent videos, you will remain my follower.   |
| in case       | 如果/以防    | **In case** you don't understand, please comment below.                |

## 让步状语从句

**even if / even though — 即使**

| 引导词 | 情况 | 例子 |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **even if**                         | 假设（不一定是事实） | **Even if I see a carrot**, I will not eat it.  |
| **although / though / even though** | 事实（让步事实）     | **Although I see a carrot**, I will not eat it. |

> **even if**：even 去掉句子照样通顺, 加上就有了让步含义; 就算看见也不吃
> **although / though / even though**：看见是事实, 但仍然不吃——事实又怎样？

⚠️ **although/though 与 but 不能连用**！因为 although 引导从句 → 已经构成复杂句。but 连接并列句 → 是复合句。两者混用 = 杂交。

**no matter + 疑问词 — 不管…（未知情况的让步）**

> **No matter what** happens, I will not eat the carrot.
> **No matter who** orders me, I will not eat the carrot.
> **No matter how** hungry I am, I will not eat the carrot.

**regardless of — 不管/无论（可替代 no matter）**

> **Regardless of** what kind of video I make, you will give it a thumbs up.

## 方式状语从句

**as if / as though — 就好像（虚拟假设）**

> I feel good **as if I had just eaten a carrot**.（与过去相反假设）
> You spent so much money **as if you were a millionaire**.（与现实相反假设）

与虚拟条件句的区别（重要对比）：

| | 方式状语从句 (as if) | 虚拟条件句 (if) |
|--|---------------------|----------------|
| 主句 | **已确认**的情况（I feel good） | **未确认**（假设结果） |
| 从句 | 与事实相反的假设补充 | 与事实相反的条件 |
| 暗示 | "我感觉良好"是真实的 | "如果吃了胡萝卜"是假设的 |

核心区别：as if 的主句是**真实发生的**, if 虚拟条件句的主句**也是假设的**。

**as — 像…一样（事实方式, 不是假设）**

> Eat the carrot **as I do**.（像我吃胡萝卜一样吃）
> Leave the carrot **as it is**.（让胡萝卜像原本那样 → 别动它）

## 原因状语从句

**because > since > as（因果/语气从强到弱）**

| 引导词 | 强度 | 含义 | 例子 |
| ----------- | ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **because** | 最强 | 实在的因果关系       | **Because** this video is excellent, you should give it a thumbs up.           |
| **since**   | 中等 | 大家都已经知道的情况 | **Since** all my videos are excellent, you should give this video a thumbs up. |
| **as**      | 最弱 | 显而易见的事实       | **As** I have thick skin, I'm begging you for a thumbs up.                     |

**语序区别**：
- because 引导的从句前后都可放
- since / as 引导的从句一般只能放主句之前

**for 不是原因状语从句！**

> You must really like me, **for you have given all my videos a thumbs up**.

**for 构成的是复合句（compound sentence）, 不是复杂句！** for 前后是两个同等地位的句子：前者推测, 后者补充推测的原因。

对比：
> **Because** you like me, you have given all my videos a thumbs up.（原因→结果, 从句）
> You must really like me, **for** you have given...（推测←原因, 并列句）

⚠️ for 不能直接替换 because！

**because 与 so 不能连用**

> ❌ Because the rabbit was hungry, so he ate the carrot.
> ✅ The rabbit was hungry, **so** he ate the carrot.

（because 构成复杂句, so 构成复合句, 不能混用。原理同 although...but）

## 目的状语从句

> **In order that** I could finish the video in time, I pulled an all-nighter.
> 为了及时完成视频这个目的, 我熬夜不睡干视频

> I pulled an all-nighter **so that** I could finish the video in time.

⚠️ so that 表目的时不可放句首。

⚠️ 目的状语从句中一般使用**情态动词**（can/could/may/might/should/would）, 体现目的性。

## 结果状语从句

**so that — 结果（与目的的区别看语境）**

> I ate a lot of carrots for lunch **so that** I wasn't hungry at all in the afternoon.（结果：不饿 → 不是目的）
> The temperature was low **so that** the lake froze.（只能是结果, 不能说"气温低是为了让湖结冰"）

**so 与 so that 的区别**

- **so**：连接两个并列句 → 复合句
  > I like this video, **so** I'll give it a thumbs up.
- **so that**：引导状语从句 → 复杂句（从句解释主句产生的结果）

**so...that — 如此…以至于**

> I ate **so many carrots** for lunch **that** I felt sick afterwards.
>（so + 形容词/副词 + that）

**such...that — 如此…以至于（such 后接名词）**

> It was **such a tasty carrot** **that** I ate it all at once.

> 如果用 so：It was **so tasty a carrot** that I ate it all at once.（偏正式/奇怪）


# 引导词总结

| 从句类型         | 引导词                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **名词从句**     | that, whether, what, who, whom, whose, which, when, where, why, how           |
| **形容词从句**   | who, whom, whose, which, that, when, where, why                               |
| **时间状语从句** | when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, the next time       |
| **地点状语从句** | where, wherever, everywhere, anywhere                                         |
| **比较状语从句** | than, as...as, the more...the more                                            |
| **条件状语从句** | if, unless, provided that, as long as, in case                                |
| **让步状语从句** | although, though, even though, even if, no matter what/who/how, regardless of |
| **方式状语从句** | as if, as though, as                                                          |
| **原因状语从句** | because, since, as（⚠️ for 不是从句引导词）                                   |
| **目的状语从句** | in order that, so that（+ 情态动词）                                          |
| **结果状语从句** | so that, so...that, such...that                                               |

## That的三种使用场景与指代逻辑


**关系代词(定语从句)**: 仅指代前面的名词先行词, 不能指代整句话。如果需要在同一句子指代前面整件事则必须使用逗号 + which
> *He passed the exam, **which** (❌ that) made his parents happy.*

**连接词(引导名词从句)**: 没有任何实际指代意义, 也不在从句中担当成分, 仅作为从句引导信号。
> *Darwin thought **that** cooking was the greatest discovery. (宾语从句)*
> *It is obvious **that** the rabbit ate a carrot. (主语从句)*

**指示代词(独立使用)**: 可以独立代指前面整句话/整件事的意思, 通常位于下一句句首做主语或宾语。
> *He passed the exam. **That** (或 This) made his parents happy.*

## Which的用法

**关系代词(限定性定语从句)**: 指代前面的名词（仅限指代“物”, 不能指代“人”）, 在从句中充当主语或宾语。
> *The carrot **which** I bought is tasty.*

**关系代词(非限定性定语从句)**: 通常位于逗号后。不仅能指代前面的名词, 还可以代指前面整句话的意思（即前面整件事）。
> *He gave me a carrot, **which** was very tasty. (指代 carrot)*
> *He passed the exam, **which** made his parents happy. (指代他通过考试整件事)*

**介词前置结构(介词 + which)**: 常用于正式书面语（如雅思学术写作）。当从句中的动词/形容词需要搭配介词时, 将介词提前至 `which` 前（如 `in which`, `to which`, `by which`）。
> *This is the way **in which** he solved the problem. (相当于 in this way)*
> *This is the extent **to which** technology affects us. (相当于 to this extent)*
> *Cheng and Wang replicated these results in a second study, **during which** they had participants complete long multiplication questions by hand. (相当于 during the study)*
