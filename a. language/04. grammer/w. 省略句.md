# 省略的核心原则

省略的目的是避免重复、使表达更简洁, 前提是省略后意思清楚不歧义。英语省略分两大类：词法省略和句法省略。

# 词法省略

| 省略类型 | 规则 | 例子 |
| ---------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 冠词       | 独一无二身份/职位前 the 可省     | Rabbit was appointed Chairman.     |
| 冠词       | 并列同冠词时第二个可省           | the rabbit and (the) wolf          |
| 介词       | 时间介词常省                     | I ate ten carrots that day.        |
| 介词       | "忙于/致力于"动作的介词可省      | I am busy making videos.           |
| 不定式     | 使役/感官动词后省 to（裸不定式） | The wolf made me watch the video.  |
| 从句引导词 | 定语从句关系代词做宾语可省       | the rabbit (that) we met yesterday |
| 从句引导词 | 宾语从句 that 可省               | I saw (that) the rabbit ate.       |

**冠词的省略**

独一无二的身份或职位前, 定冠词 the 可以省略：

> Rabbit was appointed Chairman of the Carrot Town Committee.
> Wolf served as head of our team.

并列关系中, 两个并列名词前有相同的冠词, 第二个可省略（不歧义时）：

> Both the rabbit and (the) wolf will be invited to the party.
> Is this bunny a boy or (a) girl?

⚠️ 但 and 连接两个表示身份的名词时, 有冠词和无冠词可能指不同的人：

> a teacher and video creator → 同一个人（既是老师又是视频制作者）
> a teacher and a video creator → 两个人

独立主格结构中也有冠词省略：

> The rabbit came in, carrot in hand.（= with a carrot in his hand）

**介词的省略**

时间介词常省略：

> I ate ten carrots that day.（= on that day）
> I worked 60 hours last week.（= for 60 hours in last week）

表达动作中的介词也可有可无：

> I am busy making videos.（= busy with making videos）
> There is no use crying over spilled milk.（覆水难收）

**不定式的省略**

使役动词和感官动词后不定式省略 to（裸不定式）：

> The wolf helped me eat the carrot.（= helped me to eat）
> The wolf made me watch the video.

**从句引导词的省略**

定语从句中关系代词做宾语时可省略：

> This is the rabbit (that) we met yesterday.

宾语从句中 that 可省略：

> The rabbit said (that) the carrots were tasty.

# 句法省略

**简单句中的省略**

祈使句省略主语 you：

> Open the door, please.

感叹句中的省略：

> What a smart rabbit!（省略了 he is）
> How time flies!（省略了副词 fast）

口语表达中力求简洁, 各种成分都可能省略：

> What did you eat? — A carrot.（省略 I ate）
> Who ate the carrot? — The rabbit.（省略 ate the carrot）
> Who is Papa Rabbit? — I am.（省略 Papa Rabbit）
> Would you like to eat a carrot? — Yes, I'd like to.（省略 eat a carrot, 保留 to）

**并列句中的省略**

并列句后部分重复的内容可以省略：

| 省略内容 | 例子 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 主语      | The rabbit went home after work and (the rabbit) ate a carrot.         |
| 谓语+宾语 | The rabbit has eaten the carrot, but the wolf hasn't (eaten a carrot). |
| 主语+谓语 | The story made the rabbit happy, but (the story made) the wolf sad.    |
| 谓语动词  | The rabbit likes carrots and the wolf (likes) apples.                  |

**复杂句中的省略**

从句中的省略——状语从句中, 主语与主句相同且有 be 动词时, 主语+be 一起省去：

> The rabbit will eat the carrot, but I don't know when (he will eat it).
> When (he was) young, the rabbit went to school every day.
> If (it is) possible, I'll be there on time.

比较状语从句中的省略：

> I can eat more carrots than you (can eat).
> You are smarter than I (am smart).

主句中的省略——当主句主语 I am 很明显时可省略：

> (I am) Sorry to hear that.
> Shall I give you a thumbs-up? If you'd like (you can give you a thumbs up).
