| 语法词条           | 意群                                                                                          | 释义                                                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| as (介词)        | He works **as** a teacher.                                                                  | 作为; 如同<br><br>后接名词; regard A as B 把 A 看作 B                                                                                |
| as (方式)        | Do **as** I say.                                                                            | 按照...的方式                                                                                                                  |
| as (原因)        | **As** it was late, we left.                                                                | 因为<br><br>语气比 because 弱, 常放句首                                                                                             |
| as (让步)        | Young **as** he is, he is wise.                                                             | 虽然<br><br>形容词, 副词或名词前置 + as + 主谓                                                                                          |
| as (时间)        | **As** he walked in, everyone stood up.                                                     | 当...时<br><br>强调动作同时发生                                                                                                     |
| as (非限制)       | **As** mentioned above, the plan failed.                                                    | 正如...<br><br>as 引导补充说明, 如 as is known                                                                                     |
| as (比较)        | The hall is **as** large **as** the gallery.                                                | 和...一样<br><br>第一个 as 是副词, 第二个 as 是连词或介词                                                                                   |
| as (随着)        | how wise it has proven **as** museums have come to place increasing emphasis on sponsorship | 随着; 鉴于<br><br>后接完整从句, 表逐渐发展的趋势, 并隐含原因                                                                                     |
| as (倒装)        | goal posts are typically absent, **as are** game officials or referees                      | 同样, ...也是如此<br><br>as + 助动词 + 主语 倒装, 补充说明前面的情况同样适用于另一对象, 近似 so are ...; 如 as do I, as is the case with ...                |
| how + 形容词 + 主谓 | yet **how wise** it has proven over the years                                               | 感叹结构前置, 意为多么...<br><br>how 只修饰 wise, how wise 是 has proven 的主语补语, 不修饰整句, 不能作主语<br><br>还原: it has proven (to be) very wise |
