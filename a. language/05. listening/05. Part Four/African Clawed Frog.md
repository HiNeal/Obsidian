# 错题记录

- Their skin is \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: smooth
> 错误: rough
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 录音 African Clawed Frogs have smooth skin, unlike the toad, whose skin always has a rough texture, rough 描述的是蟾蜍, 应填 smooth; 我把 unlike 之后的对比项填了进来
> 破局法则: 完成题目

- Male frogs are \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ the size of female frogs.

> 正确: half
> 错误: half big
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 录音 male frogs are about half as big as females, 题目限 ONE WORD ONLY, 只填 half; 我填了 half big, 超出字数
> 破局法则: 完成题目

- They live in a condition with a high level of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 错误: water
> 常见陷阱: 顺序倒置
> 直接原因: 录音 survive in water with high salt levels, 题干 a high level of ___ 把 salt 移到了 level 之后; 我只盯着 level 前面的 water
