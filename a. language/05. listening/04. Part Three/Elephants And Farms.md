# 错题记录

What are the TWO results that happen as elephants step on a farmer's field?

- A. farmers moved away from the affected areas
- B. some farmers' homes were destroyed
- C. farmers have less support for elephant conservation
- D. farmers tried to protect their families
- E. elephants became aggressive towards the farmers

> 正确: C. farmers have less support for elephant conservation; E. elephants became aggressive towards the farmers。原文: No, but it's a real problem because farmers are shooting the elephants to keep their crops safe, and then the other elephants are affected and become very hostile to humans. But you can see for the farmers, it's not homes and families that have been threatened.
> 错误: C. farmers have less support for elephant conservation; B. some farmers' homes were destroyed
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现; 否定干扰
> 直接原因: 选了房屋被毁, 与 not homes and families 的否定信息相反; 漏选 hostile 对应的 aggressive。
> 破局法则: 短语捕捉: 连同 not 听完整宾语, 用 hostile 对应 aggressive 后再选。

What TWO impacts could be made by bees to elephants?

- A. they can hurt the skin of the adult elephants
- B. they like to attack the sensitive parts of the elephants
- C. elephants are not afraid of bees
- D. young elephants are affected negatively
- E. they have little effect on elephants

> 正确: B. they like to attack the sensitive parts of the elephants; D. young elephants are affected negatively。原文: Yes, and then there are the baby elephants, the calves, which have thinner skin, so they can be really badly affected, especially if they get attacked by a whole swarm of bees. So elephants with babies may be threatened by bees even if they don't get stung themselves.
> 错误: B. they like to attack the sensitive parts of the elephants; A. they can hurt the skin of the adult elephants
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现; 对象限定
> 直接原因: 选了成年大象皮肤受伤, 与 can't sting through 相反; 负面影响对应皮薄的小象。
> 破局法则: 熟悉题目: 分开 adult elephants 与 calves, 听清否定后再汇总两个影响。

What is result of the experiment of hanging a container of bees by the farms?

- A. the trees with bees were trampled down by elephants
- B. the elephants started to look for the trees with bees
- C. it can be used for protecting farmer's crops

> 正确: C. it can be used for protecting farmer's crops。原文: They hung containers full of bees in trees near the farms. And they listened in trees without containers? And they found the elephants trampled down the trees that didn't have any containers. And stayed well away from the ones with the bees in.
> 错误: A. the trees with bees were trampled down by elephants
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现; 否定限定
> 直接原因: 选了有蜜蜂的树被踩踏, 原文限定被踩踏的是 didn't have any containers 的树。
> 破局法则: 短语捕捉: 把否定与其修饰的树绑定, 对照有蜜蜂与无蜜蜂两组结果。

The reason why one herd of elephants didn't run away

- A. they didn't hear the sound clear
- B. they never had the experience before
- C. they didn't pay attention to the recording

> 正确: B. they never had the experience before。原文: Maybe that herd just didn't hear it properly. It seems a very small exception to the main finding. I think researchers actually concluded that this particular herd lacked any experience of bees. They've never encountered any, so they weren't afraid of the recording.
> 错误: C. they didn't pay attention to the recording
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折; 猜测与结论
> 直接原因: 选了不注意录音, 研究者的结论是这群象从未接触过蜜蜂。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 等 actually concluded 的结论, 不把猜测或未提及的原因当答案。

What surprised the researchers when elephants heard the recording?

- A. some elephants reacted to it immediately
- B. they were scared for only 10 seconds
- C. most elephants didn't take any action

> 正确: A. some elephants reacted to it immediately。原文: Exactly. But what surprised the researchers was from the seventeen herds they did take action when they heard the recording, they found almost fifty percent of the herds did this very quickly within ten seconds of hearing the recording.

> 破局法则: 短语捕捉: 将 very quickly 和 within ten seconds 一起理解, 核对数字修饰的动作。
