**Rob**: Hello?

**Margaret**: Hello, Rob. Margaret here. I'm just calling about the ==arrangements== for Dad's eightieth birthday party.

**Rob**: Right. I've rung round the rest of the family. Most people do intend to come, even though it's a long journey. Have you made any progress with the venue?

**Margaret**: Yes, there are three possible hotels we could use for the party: The Royal, The Star, and The Winchester.

**Rob**: Oh, good. If we can make a decision now, you can probably book straight away on the Internet, can't you?

**Margaret**: Well, only The Star. I'd have to make a telephone booking at the others, but that'd be okay, too.

**Rob**: Okay. So which one do you think?

**Margaret**: I'm not sure. They're all fairly suitable, and they're in really nice locations. The Star looks right out over the ==bay==. It's on a ==cliff== top. Then The Winchester is in lovely countryside, too.

**Rob**: Yes. It's in the ==valley==, isn't it?

**Margaret**: Yes.

**Rob**: What about Mom's ==wheelchair==? How easy would it be for her to get in and out of these places?

**Margaret**: Well, I know that The Winchester would be fine, and The Star because I've been there with her before. I wasn't sure about The Royal because I seemed to remember it had a lot of ==steps==, so I checked, and apparently it's had ==ramps== installed recently, so there wouldn't be any problem there either.

**Rob**: Oh. And what about the kids? Which one do you think would be most suitable for them? Have they got play areas or anything?

**Margaret**: The Royal hasn't got anything, but The Winchester has one. It's on the ground floor, they said, not far from the dining room. The Star's got one too, but it isn't under cover, and we don't know what the weather will be like, do we?

**Rob**: Mm, true.

**Margaret**: Then there's the birthday meal itself. The Royal's the only one where there'd be a choice. The other two have a set menu at lunchtime. Mind you, they all do very good food, apparently, so the quality should be fine.

**Rob**: I don't think it really matters whether there's a ==variety== or not. But I think we need to have our own room, don't you?

**Margaret**: The only one that can provide that is The Royal. The Winchester and The Star only have one dining room, so we'd just be sitting with the other guests, but in a ==separate== area of the room.

**Rob**: Hmm. That's not quite the same, is it?

**Margaret**: No. But the other thing to consider is the price. Normally, there's not much difference between them. Perhaps The Star's ==slightly== cheaper than the other two, but The Winchester is offering quite a good reduction because there's so many of us. The Star and The Royal would just charge their standard rates.

**Rob**: Hmm.

**Rob**: So taking everything into ==consideration==, which one do you think we should go for?

**Margaret**: The Winchester, unless you-

**Rob**: Okay, that's fine by me. You've obviously gone into everything very carefully.

**Rob**: You've done nearly all the work so far, so what can I do now to help?

**Margaret**: Well, could you ==manage== the ==finances==?

**Rob**: Of course. Do they want the whole ==amount== in advance?

**Margaret**: Just a ==deposit==. I'll find out how much and let you know. Then if you could send that straight away to secure the booking.

**Rob**: Fine.

**Margaret**: And I'll go and buy some ==invitations==.

**Rob**: Oh, don't do that. I can produce them. I've got a program on my computer. It's easy.

**Margaret**: Okay. Thanks.

**Rob**: Then once we've had the replies, I'll call everyone who's coming to find out if they want ==transport==.

**Margaret**: Great. I can help out with that, pick people up from the station or whatever.

**Rob**: Okay.

**Margaret**: Oh yes, and could you stress that Dad doesn't want ==presents==? He keeps reminding me. He says there's nothing he really needs.

**Rob**: Okay. I'll try to discourage them, but you know what people are like.

**Rob**: Anything else? Shall I do-

# 错题记录

disabled access \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

- A. Royal Hotel
- B. Star Hotel
- C. Winchester Hotel
- D. all three hotels

> 正确: D. all three hotels。原文: I wasn't sure about the Royal, because I seemed to remember it had a lot of steps, so I checked. And apparently it's had ramps installed recently, so there wouldn't be any problem there either.
> 错误: B. Star Hotel
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 选了 Star 一家, 未把 Royal 最近安装坡道的信息合并进最终判断。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 等 recently 和 either 后的信息说完, 逐一核对三家酒店。

Send a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: deposit。原文: Do they want the whole amount in advance? Just a deposit. I'll find out how much and let you know. Then if you could, send that straight away to secure the booking.
> 错误: invitation
> 常见陷阱: 题干跟随
> 直接原因: 把后面制作邀请函的信息填进了当前付款事项, deposit 与 invitations 的事项对应错位。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 先锁定 finances 和 send a, 付款事项完成后再移到邀请函。

Make the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to the Winchester Hotel

> 正确: invitations。原文: And I'll go and buy some invitations. Oh, don't do that. I can produce them. I've got a program on my computer.
> 错误: invitation
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误: 单复数
> 直接原因: 写成 invitation, 与标准答案 invitations 的复数形式不符。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 将 produce them 回指 some invitations, 落笔保留复数 -s。

Find out who needs help with \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: transport。原文: Then, once we've had the replies, I'll call everyone who's coming to find out if they want transport.
> 错误: (未作答)
> 常见陷阱: 漏答; 具体听音原因需重听确认
> 直接原因: 该空未作答, 缺少 want transport 对应 help with 的信息。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 听到 once 后切换到后续安排, 错过前空也继续跟随。

Tell people not to bring any \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: presents。原文: Oh, yes. And could you stress that Dad doesn't want presents? He keeps reminding me.
> 错误: (未作答)
> 常见陷阱: 漏答; 具体听音原因需重听确认
> 直接原因: 该空未作答, 未记录与 not to bring 对应的 presents。
> 破局法则: 短语捕捉: 抓 Dad doesn't want presents 的否定主干, 先记名词再检查。
