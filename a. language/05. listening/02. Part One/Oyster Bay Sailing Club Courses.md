**Staff**: Hello, Oyster Bay Sailing Club. How can I help you?

**Caller**: Oh hi. I'd like to find out about sailing courses for beginners.

**Staff**: No problem. Is it for yourself?

**Caller**: Yes. I had a look online but I'm not sure which course would be best.

**Staff**: OK. Well you might be interested in our Taster Days?

**Caller**: Possibly.

**Staff**: So these are for people who've never sailed before – it's basically an introduction to sailing to find out whether you enjoy it and want to carry on with it.

**Caller**: And how much is that?

**Staff**: It's £120 for the day – but it's reduced to £110 each if there are two of you.

**Caller**: No, it would just be me.

**Staff**: Oh that's fine. You'd be in a small group, usually about eight people but ==no more than== ten – and everyone's always very friendly.

**Caller**: Uh huh. And are there any other suitable courses?

**Staff**: The other option is the Level 1 course. These are two-day weekend courses and we run those all year round.

**Caller**: OK. And what do you learn on that course?

**Staff**: This is a mix of theory and practical skills. So you learn about things like the ==weather==, which is obviously really important and also the ==tides==, as well as learning basic sailing skills.

**Staff**: You go out into the ==harbour== in special training ==dinghies== for beginners, two people in each dinghy and an ==instructor==. He or she will make sure you understand everything you need to know about ==safety==.

**Caller**: It sounds like hard work!

**Staff**: Yes, but you'll have a lot of fun too.

**Caller**: And the cost of that one is…?

**Staff**: £200. But it's a bit cheaper if you decide to join the club. There's a ==discount== for members.

**Caller**: Well, I'm not sure about that yet.

**Staff**: You've got plenty of time to decide.

**Caller**: And does the cost include everything?

**Staff**: Yes, everything's included and you also get a really good ==dictionary== explaining all the ==sailing terminology==. A lot of people struggle with this at first. It's got lots of pictures, so I'm sure you'd find it really helpful.

**Staff**: And ==on completion of== the course you get a ==certificate==. Then you're ready to move on to the Level 2 course.

**Caller**: Sounds good.

**Staff**: I think that's all the info you need for now. Just a couple of general things. For example, it's really important that you know how to swim.

**Caller**: Yes, I'm pretty confident in the water.

**Staff**: Great. The other thing I should tell you is that we provide ==wetsuits== and ==life jackets== but you need to bring ==swimming trunks== and some old ==trainers==.

**Caller**: And a ==towel==?

**Staff**: Yes definitely. And you might want to bring your own ==toiletries==, things like shampoo.

**Caller**: OK. What about food and drink? Do I need to bring that or is there a ==café== at the club?

**Staff**: Yes, you can get sandwiches, cakes and snacks there. The food's pretty reasonable.

**Caller**: Okay, good. Well, I think I'm interested in the level one course, but I know absolutely nothing about sailing.

**Caller**: So is there anything I can do to prepare myself a bit?

**Staff**: I recommend you watch some ==videos== we use for training. They're available online.

**Staff**: I can send you the link.

**Staff**: They'll give you an idea of what to expect.

**Caller**: Perfect, thanks. That would be very helpful.

**Caller**: Oh, and just one other thing. I'll be ==cycling== to the club and will need somewhere to put ==valuables==.

**Caller**: I'm just wondering if there are ==lockers== for people to use?

**Staff**: Yes, there are plenty in the changing rooms.

**Caller**: Great. OK well could you book me onto…

# 错题记录

| **Name of course** | **What you learn** | **Cost** | **Other information** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Taster day | introduction to sailing | £120 if booking one place | small groups (max \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ people) |

> 正确: 10/ten
> 错误: 8
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 录音 usually about eight people but no more than ten, eight 是常规人数, 题干 max 问上限, 应填 10 或 ten; 我填了 8
> 破局法则: 完成题目
