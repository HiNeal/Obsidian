**Jenny**: Hi, Greg. Great to see you. And thanks for emailing me with the details of the horseback riding trip you went on. I looked at the website, and it looks great.

**Greg**: Yeah. I went on the two-day mini break. Is that what you're thinking about?

**Jenny**: Yes, that's the one. Do you think they'd have places now in July, or will it be full?

**Greg**: If you can, hang on till September. That's when I went, and it was a really great time to go. It's not so hot, for a start.

**Jenny**: That'd suit me better anyway.

**Greg**: And also, at one point when we stopped for a picnic, I was lying in a field looking up and saw a huge flock of ==birds==. They were obviously migrating. That's another reason why it's a good time to go.

**Jenny**: Oh, great.

**Greg**: Oh, and another thing, you don't stay in a hotel. You stay in a ==tent==, so you don't have to come back to town in the evening.

**Jenny**: I'm up for that.

**Jenny**: So where do you go on the first day?

**Greg**: Through some amazing wooded valleys full of chestnut and beech trees, but that's just the morning. Where you're really heading for is the ==mountains==, and when you set out, you can only see them in the far distance. You don't actually get there until sunset.

**Jenny**: Is it hard work riding all day?

**Greg**: No. The pace is easy, and there are plenty of breaks built in for picnics and even a snooze. But on that first evening, when you've been riding all day, you get the opportunity to go for a ==swim==, and you really enjoy that. So relaxing.

**Jenny**: And what's on day two? You said something about the national park.

**Greg**: No. Day two, you go up this incredible ==valley==. At the end of it, there's a ==waterfall==. It's an amazing place to stop for lunch. We even ==spotted== an eagle while we were sitting there. And in the afternoon, there's a trip that goes to a ==cave==. You can even ride into it.

**Jenny**: Wow, that sounds great. I can't wait.

**Jenny**: Okay, so what are your tips for a horseback riding weekend?

**Greg**: Well, I'd had a sore knee before I went, and I was pretty worried about that. It was okay, actually, but what you've got to watch for is that you don't have any trouble with your ==back==. Riding can really aggravate that.

**Jenny**: No problem there. And comfortable clothes, I guess.

**Greg**: Yeah. But another thing I hadn't realized is that you actually walk the horses down some pretty ==steep== and ==uneven== paths. So make sure you have ==walking boots==, not just normal riding boots. And even if you're going in the autumn, make sure you don't get sunburned. Even if it looks cloudy, take sunscreen, lots of it.

**Jenny**: I think you've convinced me to sign up. When I was looking at the website, I saw that if I register online, I can get a ==discount==. Did you do that or go through a travel agency?

**Greg**: I ==registered== online, of course.

**Jenny**: I just have to let my boss know when I want to take time off. And then the other important thing I read was that you have to make sure that your ==insurance== company know that you're going on an adventure trip to make sure that you're covered in case anything happens.

**Greg**: I'm sure it won't, but better be safe than sorry.

# 错题记录

- Type of accommodation: a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: tent
> 错误: tant
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 录音 you stay in a tent, 空格要填 tent; 我拼成了 tant, 拼错不得分
> 破局法则: 完成题目

- Final destination: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: mountains
> 错误: mountain
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 录音 what we're heading for is the mountains, 目的地用复数 mountains, 单复数错误扣分; 我填了 mountain
> 破局法则: 完成题目

- unsuitable if you have \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ problems

> 正确: back
> 错误: bag
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: 录音先说 sore knee, 随即转折 it was okay, 要当心的是 back; 我没听清 back, 写成了 bag
> 破局法则: 短语捕捉

- rocky paths so take \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: walking boots
> 错误: walking
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 录音 make sure you have walking boots, not just normal riding boots, 空格 take ___ 要填完整名词短语 walking boots; 我只写了 walking
> 破局法则: 完成题目
