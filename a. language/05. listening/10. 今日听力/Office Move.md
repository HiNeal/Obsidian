When our ==graphic== design company was established with just the two of us ten years ago, this space was ideal and the ==rent== is very reasonable. But we've just ==outgrown== the ==space== since we took on six new staff last year. So we've decided to cut short our ==lease==.

**Manager**: I'm sure you're going to like the new office. We thought long and hard about the location and will be relocating to the ==industrial estate==, which is also where all our materials are printed. That will mean we can save on transport costs, and we should be able to pass these savings on to our customers. That way, we can keep our prices competitive as well.

**Manager**: Now, it's important that you're all aware of your responsibilities so that the move goes smoothly. We don't require you to pack your things yourselves. The movers will take care of all of that.

**Manager**: ==I know== some of you are wondering what we're going to do about all the office furniture. The new office will be completely ==fitted out==, so we don't need any of this.

**Manager**: What we've decided to do is offer it all ==for sale== at very reasonable prices. So if you or anyone on the staff knows someone who wants a chair or ==filing cabinet==, please let them know.

**Manager**: Those of you who previously stored files in your own offices are going to be sharing an open plan office.

**Manager**: So this is going to require some adjustments in how we work.

**Manager**: Over the next few weeks, I'd like you to go through your files and ==get rid of== outdated and unnecessary paperwork. There's no need to keep copies of anything.

**Manager**: Remember, things you're currently working on should stay in the office due to our privacy laws.

**Manager**: Now, the day of the move itself will be a Saturday when we are closed for business. Thank you to those who have offered to come in on that day to assist, but we'd rather you didn't. We'll leave it all to the professionals.

**Manager**: So just go directly to the new building on the Monday, and by then, hopefully, all the hard work will have been done.

**Manager**: Now, if you take a look at the plan, I'll show you the layout of our new office. Can you all see the lifts at the bottom of the plan? Now, as you come out of the lifts, you'll see the reception is on the other side of the corridor. **Manager**: You'll see some desks there ==drawn== on the plan. For the sales staff, we've asked the builders to put in a ==partition== between your area and the rest of the staff. We're aware that you're on the phone a lot talking to clients, and you'll need some privacy.

**Manager**: So that's your area in the ==top left corner==.

**Manager**: Now, if you look at the very top of the plan, ==opposite== the doorway, you'll see the designer's desks. We didn't want to put you too close to the ==entrance==. Those desks to the right of the entrance to the open plan area will be for ==temporary staff==.

# 错题记录

![[20. resource/05. listening/10. 今日听力/Office Move/picture/map.png]]

**The new office building is located close to the company's**

- A. printers.
- B. competitors.
- C. clients.

> 正确: A. printers.
> 错误: C. clients.
> 常见陷阱: 同义替换
> 直接原因: 录音 relocating to the industrial estate, which is also where all our materials are printed, 对应 A printers; 后面的 pass these savings on to our customers 是省运费的结果, 并非位置, 我被 customers 带到了 C。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 先锁定 located close to 对应的 industrial estate, 再取紧跟其后的 where all our materials are printed。

**The current office furniture will be**

- A. moved to the new office.
- B. sold to the public.
- C. given away to staff.

> 正确: B. sold to the public.
> 错误: A. moved to the new office.
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 录音 The new office will be completely fitted out, so we don't need any of this, 随即说明 What we've decided to do is offer it all for sale, 家具不搬而是出售; 我选了 A。
> 破局法则: 逻辑转折: 听到 so we don't need any of this 就排除搬运, 继续等 decided to do 之后的处理方式。

**On the day of the move, workers should**

- A. stay at home.
- B. help the movers.
- C. go to the new office.

> 正确: A. stay at home.
> 错误: C. go to the new office.
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 录音 Thank you to those who have offered to come in on that day to assist, but we'd rather you didn't, 搬家日是周六不用来; go directly to the new building 说的是 Monday, 我把周一的安排套到了搬家当天。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 先锁定 on the day of the move (Saturday), 再区分 on the Monday 的后续安排。
