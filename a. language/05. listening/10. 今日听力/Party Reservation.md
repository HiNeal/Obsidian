**Manager**: Good morning, Shaw's Restaurant.

**Maria**: Hi. I'd like to book one of your function rooms for a party, please.

**Manager**: Okay, fine. How many people are you expecting for the party?

**Maria**: Well, I'm not quite sure yet, but about sixty.

**Manager**: Fine. So you will need a big room. And what day were you thinking of?

**Maria**: The party's for my daughter's birthday. It's her twenty-first. Her birthday's actually on the seventeenth of November, but that's a Friday, so we thought we'd have her party on the eighteenth, as that's a Saturday. There'll be lots of her friends there, as well as all the family.

**Manager**: And what time would you like the room for?

**Maria**: Oh, I'm not sure. Uh, ==seven forty-five==? Not too late.

**Manager**: Okay.

**Maria**: And people will probably be leaving about eleven.

**Manager**: Fine. We're open until eleven-thirty.

**Maria**: Excellent. And what about the menu?

**Manager**: So, do you want a sit-down meal with waiter service?

**Maria**: Yes, not a buffet.

**Manager**: Right. Well, there are several options, including different menus at various prices. So there's the standard menu. That's twenty-five pounds per person.

**Maria**: Okay.

**Manager**: Or the ==celebration== menu, which is three courses. It's very popular. That's thirty-five pounds.

**Maria**: Okay. I think I'd rather go with that.

**Manager**: And there's also our gold menu. That's a five-course menu at fifty pounds.

**Maria**: No, that's definitely too expensive.

**Manager**: Okay.

**Maria**: Now, not all the guests eat meat. Will there be something for them?

**Manager**: Of course. We'll make sure there's a ==vegetarian== option for each course.

**Maria**: Great.

**Manager**: And do you know if any of the guests have food allergies? Anything like shellfish, for example?

**Maria**: I'll need to check, but I do know that one of my daughter's friends is very allergic to ==strawberries==. Even just being near them can be a problem.

**Manager**: Okay. I'll make a note to our chef to avoid those completely. Do you have any other requests for desserts?

**Maria**: Well, I'd like a choice if possible. And could we have ==cheese== as well?

**Manager**: Of course. No problem. We can provide that.

**Manager**: Now, do you have any other requests I could help with? What about after-dinner entertainment? You can book a band if you'd like live music.

**Maria**: Actually, I think a ==disco== would be best, if you can arrange that.

**Manager**: No problem.

**Maria**: And I heard you might be able to put on a fireworks display. Is that possible?

**Manager**: We aren't offering that service anymore, I'm afraid. It was just too expensive.

**Maria**: Oh, I see.

**Manager**: Now, the menu package you've chosen includes a few other nice things.

**Maria**: Oh, like what?

**Manager**: We'll decorate the room for you, which creates a nice party atmosphere. We can use different colors for the balloons: silver, gold-

**Maria**: She loves pink, but actually, I think she'd prefer ==flowers== to balloons. So let's go for them instead in the same color.

**Manager**: No problem. Now, you mentioned that the party's for your daughter. We make birthday cakes if you'd like one.

**Maria**: Okay, yes. We'll definitely want a cake.

**Manager**: And we can put a special message on top. What would you like?

**Maria**: Um, ==congratulations==, and then her name. That's Susie.

**Manager**: Fine.

**Maria**: Now, one or two of the guests are coming by train, but they stop running quite early, so they'll need a ==taxi== to take them to the Forest Hotel after the party. Is that something you can sort out?

**Manager**: Absolutely. Anything else?

**Maria**: I think that's all for now.

**Manager**: Okay. Well, just get in touch if you think of anything else. Now, if I can just take some contact details, I just need your name and phone number.

**Maria**: I'm Maria Nelson, and my number's oh seven seven four nine one three two seven six oh.

**Manager**: Excellent. We look forward to seeing you soon.

# 错题记录

Starting time of the party: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ pm

> 正确: 7:45。原文: And what time would you like the room for? Oh, I'm not sure. Uh, seven forty-five? Not too late. And people will probably be leaving about eleven.
> 错误: 7:30
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: seven forty-five 听成了 seven thirty, 后面的 eleven 与 eleven-thirty 是离场和关门时间, 与开始时间无关。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 数字时间要听完整个数词, 复核 forty-five 与 thirty 的区别。

The \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ($35)

> 正确: celebration。原文: Or the celebration menu, which is three courses. It's very popular. That's thirty-five pounds.
> 错误: celebrate
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误: 词形
> 直接原因: 价格对应到了 celebration menu, 但我把名词 celebration 写成了动词形式 celebrate。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 空格在 The ___ ($35) 的 menu 前, 需要名词修饰词, 落笔写与原文完全一致的词形。

Each course has a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ option.

> 正确: vegetarian。原文: Now, not all the guests eat meat. Will there be something for them? Of course. We'll make sure there's a vegetarian option for each course.
> 错误: vegetrian
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 听到了 vegetarian, 但拼写漏掉字母 a。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 长词落笔后逐字母复核拼写。

One guest is allergic to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

> 正确: strawberries。原文: I do know that one of my daughter's friends is very allergic to strawberries. Even just being near them can be a problem.
> 错误: strawberry
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误: 单复数
> 直接原因: 原文是复数 strawberries, 后面还有代词 them 回指, 我写成了单数 strawberry。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 以 them 等回指词判断单复数, 落笔保留复数 -ies。

Desserts and \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: cheese。原文: Well, I'd like a choice if possible. And could we have cheese as well? Of course. No problem.
> 错误: chisel
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: 没有听清 cheese, 写成了读音相近的 chisel, 也不符合 desserts and 后需要的食物名称。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 听音存疑时用题干语义校验, desserts and ___ 要填食物名词。

Entertainment: book a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ after dinner

> 正确: disco。原文: You can book a band if you'd like live music. Actually, I think a disco would be best, if you can arrange that.
> 错误: band
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: band 是服务员的提议, 随即被 Actually, I think a disco would be best 改掉, 我填了被否定的 band。
> 破局法则: 逻辑转折: 听到 Actually 就以后面的最终选择为准, 不抢填第一个出现的选项。

Special request: write \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ 'Susie' on the birthday cake

> 正确: congratulations。原文: And we can put a special message on top. What would you like? Um, congratulations, and then her name. That's Susie.
> 错误: congraulations
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 听到了 congratulations, 但拼写漏掉字母 t。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 长词落笔后逐字母复核拼写。
