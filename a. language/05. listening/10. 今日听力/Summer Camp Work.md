# 错题记录

Job in summer camp lasts for \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ weeks with the chance to travel afterwards

> 正确: eight。原文: Now, about times, the ad said the job was for twelve weeks? Eight, actually, but most people stay on in the US for another three or four weeks and go traveling.
> 错误: three
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 对方先用 twelve weeks 提问, 随即用 Eight, actually 纠正, 我把后面旅行时间 another three or four weeks 里的 three 填了进来。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 听到 actually 这类纠正词, 以更正后的数字为准, 再核对它是否对应题干的 job lasts for。

First week of job is used for \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: training。原文: You get there a week before the kids arrive, and that time's spent on training, so you'll learn all about the camp and working with American children and dealing with homesickness.
> 错误: trip
> 常见陷阱: 同义替换
> 直接原因: 题干 first week 在原文变成 a week before the kids arrive, 没跟上这一替换, 把前文 go traveling 的内容当成了答案。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 先锁定 a week before the kids arrive, 再取紧跟其后 spent on 的名词 training。

Salary: $\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: 985。原文: You get a total of nine hundred and eighty-five US dollars. You get a couple of hundred dollars more if you're over twenty-one.
> 错误: 980
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: nine hundred and eighty-five 末尾的 five 没听完整, 写成了 980。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 数字要听完整个数词再落笔, 并复核 eighty 与 eighty-five 的区别。

A \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ check in UK (£36)

> 正确: police。原文: You have to be checked by the police here in the UK, and that costs thirty-six pounds.
> 错误: plice
> 常见陷阱: 拼写错误
> 直接原因: 听到了 police, 但拼写漏掉字母 o。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 常见词落笔后逐字母复核拼写。
