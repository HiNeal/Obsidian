**Paul**: Good morning, madam. I lost my travel bag on the train. I was wondering if you could help me.

**Staff**: Of course. Sit down, please.

**Paul**: Thank you very much.

**Staff**: First, let me take some details. What's your name, please?

**Paul**: My name is Paul Smithers.

**Staff**: Smithers. Could you please spell it?

**Paul**: Paul Smithers: S-M-I-T-H-E-R-S.

**Staff**: All right. And when did you find you lost your travel bag?

**Paul**: Yesterday evening. I just finished a whole day's paperwork and took the train to return home. I was completely exhausted.

**Staff**: Yesterday. Today is the fifteenth of July, so it's the fourteenth. You said you were on the train when you lost your bag. Do you remember the number of the coach? Which coach were you in?

**Paul**: What do you mean?

**Staff**: Each coach has different letters represented its unique functions. For example, coach A is for smoking ==passengers==, while coach B is for non-smoking passengers. So do you still remember the number of the coach?

**Paul**: I don't remember the exact letter, but I do recall that I was trying to relax my brain with a cup of coffee. So it must be the ==buffet== car.

**Staff**: Wait a minute. I'll check it up for you. You mean the buffet car? That's the coach E.

**Paul**: Oh, yes. That's it then.

**Staff**: All right. Now tell me something about the bag. What's the color?

**Paul**: It is a brown one with a small blue carton race car image at the bottom in the left-hand corner.

**Staff**: Okay, I know. And what does it look like? What's the style?

**Paul**: What do you mean?

**Staff**: What kind of distinctive features does it have? Like, is it a box type or a soft one? Does it contain any pockets?

**Paul**: Well, it's a soft one on the ==rack==. What's more, there are ==two pockets== at the front. They have long straps and are made by nylon.

**Staff**: Right. Got that. Did you lose any other things besides your bag?

**Paul**: Uh, my jacket, which is made up of ==leather==, and my ==notebook== were in it.

**Staff**: I am sorry to hear it. Do you remember where did you exactly put them?

**Paul**: Well, I walked in the coach, sat down beside my table, ordered my coffee, then, uh, yeah, I found there was not enough space to put my stuff. So I put them by the door, and I forgot to take them when I leave.

**Staff**: Okay, I know. So how much do you think the lost item cost?

**Paul**: Well, my travel bag was about one hundred pounds, but the leather jacket cost me three hundred forty-five pounds, and the notebook, it is not worth much itself, but it contains all the contact I need. It's so frustrating.

**Staff**: We also need your personal information.

**Paul**: No problem. Please.

**Staff**: Where do you live?

**Paul**: Twenty-one Kierke Street. K-I-E-R-K-E Street, Canterbury.

**Staff**: Kierke Street. My cousin also lives there, and I went there sometimes. A quiet street, isn't it?

**Paul**: Yes, indeed. Have you ever been to the park at the corner of the street end? It's very quiet and beautiful. My aunt and uncle like there so much that they go there almost every week.

**Staff**: Not yet, but I'll certainly try there sometime. Uh, let me see. Where were we? Oh, yes, your post code.

**Paul**: GW four three two HA.

**Staff**: Phone number.

**Paul**: Three two one double eight nine two.

**Staff**: Okay. I think that's all we need at the moment. We'll contact you as soon as we have any information on them.

**Paul**: Thank you very much.

**Staff**: You're welcome.

**Paul**: Bye.

**Staff**: Bye.

# 错题记录

Last time noted the lost item: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: 14 July (或 14th July / July 14 / July 14th)。原文: Yesterday. Today is the fifteenth of July, so it's the fourteenth.
> 错误: 14th
> 常见陷阱: 细节漏写
> 直接原因: 听出了日期是 14 号, 但漏写了月份 July, 只填了 14th, 日期表达不完整。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 日期类填空需同时写全数字和月份, 听清 fifteenth of July 后立刻补全 14 July。

Description of the lost item (main features): a soft bag on the rack / \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: 2 pockets。原文: Well, it's a soft one on the rack. What's more, there are two pockets at the front.
> 错误: blue
> 常见陷阱: 题干跟随
> 直接原因: 听到前面包的颜色描述 small blue carton race car image 时误填了 blue, 没有等到 What's more 引出的正面有两个口袋(two pockets)的特征。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 看到 main features 下的第二个并列点, 顺着 What's more 抓取数量与特征名词 2 pockets。

There was a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ jacket and a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ inside the bag

> 正确: leather。原文: Uh, my jacket, which is made up of leather, and my notebook were in it.
> 错误: browse
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: 录音用定语从句后置修饰 my jacket, which is made up of leather, 未适应后置材质表达且受前文 brown 音近干扰, 写成了无关词 browse。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 预判 ___ jacket 为材质形容词或名词, 听到 jacket 后紧跟 which is made up of 后的材质词 leather。

There was a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ jacket and a \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ inside the bag

> 正确: notebook。原文: Uh, my jacket, which is made up of leather, and my notebook were in it.
> 错误: table
> 常见陷阱: 题干跟随
> 直接原因: 夹克与笔记本在同一句并列出现, 错过并列项 and my notebook, 把后面叙述中出现的 sat down beside my table 里的 table 填了进来。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 遇到 a ___ and a ___ 并列结构时保持连续听音, 紧抓 jacket 后面的 and my notebook。

Address: 21 \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Street, Canterbury

> 正确: Kierke。原文: Twenty-one Kierke Street. K-I-E-R-K-E Street, Canterbury.
> 错误: Kaeri
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: 街道名由说话人逐字母拼读 K-I-E-R-K-E, 记录字母拼写时中间几个字母听混, 写成了 Kaeri。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 遇到专名拼读时集中注意力逐个字母速记, 复核 ie 与 rk 的拼写顺序。

Post code: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

> 正确: GW432HA。原文: GW four three two HA.
> 错误: gw43a
> 常见陷阱: 听音偏差
> 直接原因: 邮编由字母与数字混合组成(GW 4 3 2 HA), 漏记了数字 2 与字母 H。
> 破局法则: 完成题目: 邮编通常为字母+数字+字母固定组合, 边听边按位核对完整位数(7 位)避免漏字符。
