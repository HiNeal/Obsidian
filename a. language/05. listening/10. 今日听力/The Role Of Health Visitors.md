**Dr. Harris**: Hi, Lisa. Good to see you again, especially since you've chosen to do your master's with us.

**Lisa**: Well, I enjoyed my 1st degree in nursing, and I decided I want to become a health visitor. You know, someone who promotes health and wellbeing and is part of a team within a community, getting out and meeting people of all ages. So that's why I chose this research project.

**Dr. Harris**: Excellent. And your focus, the role of a health visitor in the community, is spot on. But at this stage, you need to keep an open mind so you can respond to initial findings once you get underway.

**Lisa**: From what I read, the role seems to vary quite considerably.

**Dr. Harris**: Yes. Some health visitors tend to focus on liaising with family doctors. Others work independently with new patients. The work of health visitors in future is most likely to rely on the relationship between these roles, and I think that would be an interesting angle for you to pursue at some stage.

**Lisa**: Yes. What I really want to impact is the problem of physical development checks, which start with babies when they are only a few days old.

**Dr. Harris**: Right.

**Lisa**: Another area which I am thinking of investigating is the monitoring of nurseries, which is currently under review. And there is the issue of preschools, at what age they should take very young children. I thought I'd also include child minders, the individuals in the area of Haynes that might not be qualified, but look after young children.

**Dr. Harris**: That's an area I'd stay clear of, as it's very complex.

**Lisa**: Okay, will do. Anyway, I'd also like to make a short film about a few children.

**Dr. Harris**: A good idea, but it will depend on building up a relationship with one or 2 health visitors willing to introduce you to a family. An opportunity like that would allow you to look at how young children begin to form words, for example.

**Lisa**: And peer relationships between children, as well as the ways in which they relate and play with each other, especially when they are around 2 to 3 years old, although early use of language is my main interest.

**Dr. Harris**: Okay, but there are other topic areas I'd like you to consider before we finalize the content and structure of your research. An equally important area is the child's home environment and the effect of a newborn baby.

**Lisa**: Right.

**Dr. Harris**: You need to consider the impact of this on the parents, particularly if the family lives in poor economic circumstances. Of course, the additional expense could be a worry, especially if there are other children. Certainly, all those matters are very sensitive and not necessarily easy to detect. There's plenty of help available, but many people often feel too proud to ask for it.

**Lisa**: This is the area where I will need to be familiar with family law, isn't it?

**Dr. Harris**: Very much so. By all means, be aware of the medical conditions, and you also need to consider social practice. But you'll find that in most circumstances, you will have to refer to the law to understand how far a health visitor can intervene.

**Lisa**: Yes, I appreciate that.

**Dr. Harris**: Now, your book list. I'm glad to see Worldwide Health Care Policies tops your list.

**Lisa**: Well, I wasn't sure how relevant it would be, but it's a new edition, so the information is current.

**Dr. Harris**: Yes, some of it may not be relevant, but it's important to keep abreast of developments in other countries which may inform our own government's legislation.

**Lisa**: I understand. What about Supporting Vulnerable Families? It's not as costly as some of the other books on my list.

**Dr. Harris**: It's an excellent buy considering the price, even though it does leave a few things out.

**Lisa**: I've already ordered Health Visitors and Child Protection. I hope that was right.

**Dr. Harris**: Well, it's very expensive, but it's a sound investment, and there aren't many textbooks which cover as much detail as this. It will be a valuable reference, even if the style is occasionally boring.

**Lisa**: And what about The Future of Health Visiting? I'm unsure how useful it will be.

**Dr. Harris**: Useful in a utilitarian sense? Probably not. But it's stimulating in the way the writer considers what health visiting could be like in a perfect world. I'd suggest dipping into it to discover the improvements which could be made if money were no object.

**Lisa**: And Resources for Health Visitors?

**Dr. Harris**: Strangely enough, it's not as helpful as the title suggested. It offers a huge variety of activities and strategies which are great when working with young children, but they're presented in a rather haphazard way, and the index isn't much use either. Be very careful when using it.

**Lisa**: Okay.

# 错题记录

**In the context of Lisa's research project, Dr. Harris suggests she should?**

- A. re-think the focus of her research
- B. be prepared to be flexible
- C. narrow the scope

> 正确: B. be prepared to be flexible
> 错误: A. re-think the focus of her research
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 导师先肯定 your focus ... is spot on(方向准确, 排除 A), 随后用 But 转折提出 you need to keep an open mind so you can respond to initial findings, 对应 B 的灵活应变。
> 破局法则: 逻辑转折: 听到 spot on 后立刻留意 But 引出的核心建议 keep an open mind, 对应 flexible。

**According to Dr. Harris, how might the arrival of a new baby affect a family?**

- A. Other children may feel jealous.
- B. Parents may be put under pressure.
- C. Sufficient support may not be available.

> 正确: B. Parents may be put under pressure.
> 错误: C. Sufficient support may not be available.
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现
> 直接原因: 录音 You need to consider the impact of this on the parents ... the additional expense could be a worry 对应 B; 紧接着明确说 There's plenty of help available, but many people often feel too proud to ask for it, 否定并非没有支持, 我误选了 C。
> 破局法则: 逻辑转折: 听到 plenty of help available 就排除 C, 回归前句 on the parents 与 worry 锁定 B。

**Dr. Harris agrees with Lisa that she needs to find out about**

- A. legal constraints.
- B. family considerations.
- C. medical circumstances.

> 正确: A. legal constraints.
> 错误: B. family considerations.
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现
> 直接原因: Lisa 问 where I will need to be familiar with family law, 导师回答 Very much so, 并在转折句强调 you will have to refer to the law to understand how far a health visitor can intervene, 对应 A; medical conditions 只是陪衬项。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 紧跟 agrees with Lisa 对应的提问 family law 与回答 Very much so, 不被中间并列的 medical conditions 和 social practice 带偏。
