**Speaker**: Welcome to this talk about the UK Summer Sports Camp. I'm going to tell you about our summer sports camp for children and something about the children who come. Later on, I'll tell you how to apply if you'd like to work with us this year. So please be patient. Feel free to interrupt if there's anything you don't understand about the way the camp is administered, though if you could save any questions about the work itself until the end, that would be good.

**Speaker**: The UK Summer Sports Camp offers ==courses== for kids aged six to sixteen, which focus on a wide range of sports and activities.

**Speaker**: Our aim is to provide a fun, safe experience for kids.

**Speaker**: The reason we're successful is because parents trust us and send their children to us summer after summer. We aren't the cheapest camp around, uh, which might put some people off, but we make sure our team are caring and enthusiastic, and that's why parents want their children to keep coming back rather than because of the facilities on offer. Great as they are, of course.

**Speaker**: We think our summer camp is special in a number of ways. We have students from all over the country, not just the local area. We run all the activities you'd expect in a summer camp, arts and music as well as sports, but we also have special coaching for those who might be able to make a ==career== in their chosen sport.

**Speaker**: What will your role be if you come and work with us then? We need lots of people who are willing to teach the activities, but we also have non-coaching roles. What we particularly like this summer are people who are willing to be in charge of looking after our younger kids, making sure they're happy and have someone to go to if they have any problems. We usually need a few cooks too, though we don't need anyone ==extra== for our office at the moment.

**Speaker**: This year, I'm very pleased to introduce a new role at the camp: camera person. We need someone with great video and photography skills to record the fun everyone has. We'll supply first-class equipment, but we need you to supply your creative abilities. If you're interested in this role, come and talk to me at the end.

**Speaker**: Now, you probably want to know about working conditions, hours, and pay. Um, this depends on your role. You can choose to work daytimes, evenings, or weekends. Daytime coaching roles pay the highest ==wages== because of the sports skills required and the longer hours. If you choose to work in the kitchen, your hours of work will be either at breakfast and lunch or at dinner. Cleaners work in the mornings before the camp activities start at ten AM, so not too early.

**Speaker**: If you do work with us, there are lots of benefits in addition to the money. Whether you're teaching a sport or not, you'll be given access to all our facilities, which means you can have a swim or play a football match without having to pay anything. We have an Internet cafe where there's a small charge for access, and, uh, we also provide discounted meals in our canteen. We'll give everyone UK summer camp T-shirts, which we'll expect you to wear when working, and if you wish, we also have some cool sports bags which are available for a small fee. So what do we expect from you?

**Speaker**: You'll be glad to hear that we don't need to see any formal certificates. If you've worked with kids before, that would be an advantage, but it isn't essential.

**Speaker**: Of course, you need to be sociable and enjoy meeting people.

**Speaker**: There'll always be a ==supervisor== available who you should talk to before making any important decisions. We can't accept applications from people under the age of eighteen, I'm afraid. But if you haven't got there yet, don't worry. There's always next time.

# 错题记录

**The speaker says that he is happy to answer questions during his talk about**

- A. the general operation of the camp
- B. the children who attend
- C. the job responsibilities

> 正确: A. the general operation of the camp
> 错误: C. the job responsibilities
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现
> 直接原因: 录音 Feel free to interrupt if there's anything you don't understand about the way the camp is administered 对应 A; 后半句 though if you could save any questions about the work itself until the end 明确要求把工作职责的问题留到最后, C 是讲座途中不可问的干扰项。
> 破局法则: 逻辑转折: 听到 though if you could save ... until the end 立刻排除 C, 回头确认 during his talk 允许提问的是 the way the camp is administered。

**One special thing about this summer camp is**

- A. the number of international students
- B. the preparation for professional sports
- C. the inclusion of non-sports activities

> 正确: B. the preparation for professional sports
> 错误: A. the number of international students
> 常见陷阱: 选项全现
> 直接原因: 录音 but we also have special coaching for those who might be able to make a career in their chosen sport 对应 B; 原文只说 students from all over the country(全国各地而非国际学生), arts and music 也是常规期待项目(you'd expect), A、C 均不成立。
> 破局法则: 题干跟随: 留意 special 信号词对应的 but we also have special coaching, 同时区分 all over the country 与 international 的范围差异。

**Which TWO things do applicants need in order to get a job at the camp?**

- A. experience of working with children
- B. an ability to make decisions on their own
- C. a friendly personality
- D. relevant qualifications
- E. to be over a certain age

> 正确: C. a friendly personality; E. to be over a certain age
> 错误: B. an ability to make decisions on their own; C. a friendly personality
> 常见陷阱: 逻辑转折
> 直接原因: 录音 We can't accept applications from people under the age of eighteen 对应 E; B 与原文 There'll always be a supervisor available who you should talk to before making any important decisions 相反(做重要决定前要找主管沟通, 不是独立决定)。
> 破局法则: 选项全现: 多选题逐项核对限定条件, 听到 talk to before making any important decisions 即排除 B, 顺延听完年龄限制 under the age of eighteen 锁定 E。
